# Calamity lore: who is still alive out there (for Warbands)

Source: Calamity's own text (Items.Lore = Yharim's voice; Bestiary + Bestiary.Vanilla), read from the CalamityMod 2.2.x localization. Quotes are paraphrased; names are canon.

## Factions that plausibly survive, with a reason to fight you
- **Wulfrum rebels** (Forest/surface, pre-boss): Wulfrum machines were built by those who rebelled against Yharim; the Drone "harassed the basic troopers of Yharim's forces", the Rover is "the honorary mascot of those who rebelled". Scrap engineers with drones. They'd see an army-raising warlord (you) as the next Yharim.
- **Yharim's remnant forces** (Tundra, early HM): Rimehounds were bred as tundra scouts and abandoned; "what remained of Yharim's forces" hunted the elementals after Azafure fell. Deserters and veterans with nothing left but the war.
- **Azafure's warlocks** (Brimstone Crag / Underworld): warlocks remain in Azafure and animate Despair Stones so "none dare trespass on their masters' lands". Intruder rule: you walk in, they burn you.
- **Ilmerian survivors** (Beach / Sunken Sea / Desert): a constitutional monarchy under the Electric Goddess Otonilou and the God of the Seas; they held Yharim back by electrifying the water (Ghost Bells, Stormlions still carry the charge); Cnidrions were the war-steeds of honored Ilmerian warriors and still guard the old heart of the kingdom → mounted bands.
- **Onyx Kinsmen** (Underground / Dungeon): the clan that worships the Slime God for keeping balance; trade with Signus ("for any desire, there is a price"). Bone Lee was one of them. Mysterious, deal-makers, assassins.
- **The Dragon Cult** (Dungeon / Lunatic Cultist): sworn to keep the behemoth sealed on the moon and to resurrect a dragon; furious about the Polterghast. They'd hate you for killing the creatures and bosses they tend.
- **Profaned faithful** (Underworld / Profaned temples, post-ML): Providence promised to end inequality by reducing all to featureless ash; her worshippers "saw little value in life".
- **Vernal Pass villagers** (Jungle, post-Plantera): Silva's people, home of her champion Braelor; they shackled souls to make Plantera as a guardian against Yharim. A jungle band with Life Magic (the Dryad's art, pioneered by Silva) paying out in Chlorophyte fits perfectly.
- **Draedon's scientists** (labs): "He and his scientists" worked in the underground labs; recon bodies of Draedon still walk around.

## Answers to Eve's questions
- **What did Silva do?** Nothing specific is ever shown. She is "the goddess of life itself"; Yharim killed her and dumped her remains in the Abyss, where she "refused to truly die" and keeps growing (she drove Anahita out, keeps the Sulphurous Sea's life alive, burrowed toward the underworld). His case is categorical: in Requiem, the first god Xeroc consumed the dying Light Dragon Zeratros' auric soul, others copied him, and the gods hunted the impure dragons — "good intentions or no, all gods are sinners, each and every one complicit in genocide". Crabulon's entry hints at his personal disgust for life that won't let the dead rest ("forceful exertion of control over the unwilling").
- **Was Yharim justified?** The narrator is Yharim himself, and he's unusually self-critical: the Crimson and Corruption are his "first mistake" (god essence left to rot); he left the Dungeon prisoners to become the Polterghast "paralyzed by apathy"; recruiting the Devourer was "the moment my descent began"; Permafrost left because "where justice was once seen, tyranny had blinded it". But he refused to eat Yharon's soul and become God-King ("Destiny is for the weak"), was thrown in the lava for it, and broke with Draedon over enslaving the Plague bees. A crusader with a real grievance who became a tyrant.
- **Why burn Ilmeris?** Strategic, per the bestiary: Ilmeris fought him under its sea gods and electrified its waters "so that none could cross their borders"; Ghost Bells and trained sand sharks held his armies back. The Witch was the one weapon that could break that line — "the Brimstone Witch turned her hexes against the kingdom" in the Great Incineration, and the sand sharks "were barely even a distraction". That act is likely her "horrific sin".
- **Azafure** wasn't burned by Yharim: the Brimstone Elemental woke (rumours: brimstone over-mining, pollution, or a deliberate plot) and Calamitas, who grew up there (her brothers were Azafurians), fought her to a standstill; the city burned in between.
