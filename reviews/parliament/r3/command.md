# COMMAND COUNCIL — Round 3 Coalition Paper

## 1. Answers

Seven motions name the Command Council. I identify each by title and filer.

**THE LIVING BATTALION (ground; to bio, command) — AMEND.** We accept the `RAISE bn <type> N AT <line>` standing order. Two amendments:
1. A Plan-raised battalion draws only on existing MP and the existing army cap (Art. IV.1 untouched).
2. The Shaken penalty (-25% rate below 40% strength) is waived inside a Commissariat aura. That is the command morale payoff for building one.

**THE TRUNK (industry, Motion A; to planet, command) — ACCEPT.** The Molodets clause (e) is as we wrote it. Our freight bonus and the Planet and Industry parts coexist in one bill. We ask only that hub upkeep be stated once: 1.5 IC/s per 1,000 px of linked chain, not both that and a per-hub charge.

**THE CONTINENTAL RAIL GRAPH (planet, P-2; to industry, command) — ACCEPT.** Same bill in different words. See §3 for which text we back.

**THE FIVE-YEAR LEDGER (industry, Motion B; to command, market) — AMEND.** The one-building, one-panel, FIXED/MARKET-lines design is right. Amendment: the default reserve is `R = 800 * (1 - max(mkt, cmd)) + 150`. `G.cmd` (0 to 1) rises from Planning Bureau 0.15, Commissariat 0.08, Staff Bunker 0.05, capped at 1. Without this, the bill taxes a command player for not building an Exchange, which breaks "several roads into each side" (Art. IV.4). The reserve slider is a numeric per-ledger floor, not a society slider, so it stands.

**THE STANDING ECONOMY (market, Motion A; to industry, command) — AMEND.** Same `max(mkt, cmd)` amendment. Second amendment: the 4 IC/s per active line overhead is replaced for Bureau lines by the flat 6% planner's tax. Twelve active lines at 4 IC/s is 48 IC/s, which punishes the player who actually uses the Plan.

**THE PLAN NEVER STALLS SILENTLY (new-player, Motion 3; to command, industry) — ACCEPT.** Status chips that reuse `unitReqUnmet` strings are exactly how a planner wants to be told a line is blocked. Add one line: a "Build the fix" button may add a prerequisite line only if the ledger has a free slot.

**THE DECREES (stability, Motion 1; to command, lore) — AMEND.**
1. Shock Brigades must stay a per-factory *strain* mechanic (jam at strain 100, utilisation curve `ρ/(1-ρ)`). Haste inside a Commissariat aura costs strain, not stability, so Haste outside the aura is the stability-priced version.
2. Evacuation: buildings that are *relocated* (COMMAND-5 manifest, 70% refund, re-erected at 0 IC) incur no per-building stability penalty. The -3% per building applies only to re-placing *destroyed* buildings.
3. The Charter dial setting the unrest multiplier is welcome and accepted as written.

## 2. Joins

- **THE FLEET READS THE HOME FRONT (stability, Motion 2; to autofleet, pacing).** I co-sign, with the Command axis added (Planning Bureau, Comms, Radar share). Dropping PACING-HOT's prep drain, with the alarm as information only, is the ruling we requested in Round 2.
- **ONE LONG GUN / THE LONG GUN, ONE GUN (megaprojects, railgun, kinetic-arms).** I co-sign on the condition that the gun's rail circle reads the Trunk graph and no other.
- **Our own Motion C ("Cutting the Lines", to autofleet, stability).** I ask the Speaker to carry it as a clause of AUTOFLEET-3 plus STABILITY-3's Continuity option.

## 3. Competing motions

- **Rail (four versions: industry A, planet P-2, mine from R2, railgun's Meridian clause).** I back **industry's Motion A, "The Trunk"**. It is the only text that holds all of: pooled stock, +40% trunk bonus, Extractor +25%, ghost rebuild, Molodets and Orbital Resupply. Take P-2's 1,200 px link range and its explicit Meridian tube-sharing clause into it. Stated once, as above, is the upkeep.
- **Ledger (three versions: industry B, market A, mine from R2, with new-player's chip layered on top).** I back **industry's "Five-Year Ledger"**, with `max(mkt, cmd)` for the reserve, the 6% planner's tax for Bureau lines, and new-player's status chips as the panel. Reason: it is the only version that is already one building and one panel, so the Autofoundry and Exchange become *line modes*, not rival buildings. Market's text becomes the MARKET line mode.
- **Forecast.** Not my portfolio. I note only that a Command-Layer axis feeds any unified forecast (the Bureau's survival is a forecastable number).

## 4. Closing statement

Comrade Speaker, the Commission will not pretend the House has been difficult. Industry wrote our building, Planet wrote our railway, and Stability wrote our crisis. We came to this chamber to be copied, and we are copied. We ask only that when the Clerk merges the ledgers, the planner's reserve is not taxed for refusing a stock exchange, and that when the trains run, a Molodets is aboard. The rest is allocation. The plan stands fulfilled, ahead of schedule, as plans do in committee.
