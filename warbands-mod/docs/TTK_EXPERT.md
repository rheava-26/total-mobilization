# Warbands: time-to-kill in Expert (lab 2)

Questions from Eve, all in **Expert** (not Master), with on-tier gear at four stages: how fast hostile levies would kill a player, how hard a hostile levy is to kill, and how both compare with ordinary enemies and with vanilla PvP. Phase 1 (`TTK.md`) was arithmetic; this lab runs the real game code. Lab code: `levy-mod/Levy/Common/Testing/CombatHarness.HostileLab.cs`, specs `levy-mod/lab/specs/ttk_expert_*.yaml` (generated by `gen_ttk_expert.py`), summaries `levy-mod/lab/ttk_expert_summary.py` and `ttk_expert_doc.py`. Raw rows (gzipped, with the cell tables) are in `warbands-mod/docs/ttk_expert_raw/`; the lab install and runs under /home/user/lab/kit_wb2 were deleted after use.

## Summary

**Fair** (a player should win by playing): ranged hostile levies at stages 0 to 3 land 15 to 65 damage per second on a 200 to 400 HP player and kill in 4 to 19 seconds. That is the same band as ordinary Expert enemies of the stage (Demon 44, Hellbat 50, Chaos Elemental 45 dps; Zombie, Skeleton, Goblin Archer 2 to 14). Three levies at once are barely worse than one (TTK down 5 to 25%), because the player's own 40-tick immunity frames cap every attacker together at about 1.3 to 1.5 landed hits per second. Fast weak weapons (Minishark, Clockwork Assault Rifle, Megashark, Crystal Storm, Nettle Burst) never kill a defended player inside 30 seconds: their 10 to 20 damage shots lose most of their size to defense and then the i-frames throw away the rest.

**Deletion**: any single big hit. The Sniper Rifle (157 damage as a levy fires it) lands 222 per hit on a 55-defense player and kills 500 HP in 2 to 3.5 seconds; one levy is enough. The Terra Blade levy (79 per hit) kills in 5 s. The early-Hardmode Mythril Repeater (46 per hit) kills 400 HP in 6 to 7 s and the Musket (32 per hit) in 13 to 15 s. The cause is not the damage rule, it is that a levy's hit is a weapon's full hit with no per-stage ceiling, and that i-frames make every hit count in full while weak hits count for nothing. Ordinary enemies at the same stage do 14 to 130 per hit; only the Skeleton Sniper (134 per hit, 0.1 to 0.2 hits per second) matches the Sniper levy per hit and it fires about once in 7 seconds.

**Without i-frames the same levies are deletion everywhere**: three Minishark levies kill a 200 HP player in 1.4 s, Phoenix Blaster in 0.8 s, Megashark in 3.2 s, Sniper Rifle in 1.0 s. The immunity frames are the only thing standing between the player and these weapons, so any rule that changes them (hostile hits ignoring them, a shared immunity per source, a longer window) changes everything.

**A sensible rule** (not implemented, for Eve to decide):

1. Keep the player's vanilla immunity (40 ticks, 20 if the hit was 1 damage, 80/40 with Cross Necklace). Do not give hostile levies their own frames and do not stack attackers' frames; the cap is what makes three levies survivable. Cross Necklace roughly halves landed hits (0.75 per second) and turns most fast-weapon bands into harmless ones; that is a fine reward.
2. Cap a levy's landed hit, not its dps: about 12% of the typical life of the stage (24 / 36 / 48 / 60 damage after the player's defense, at 200 / 300 / 400 / 500 HP), with the Sniper allowed 25%. Ordinary Expert enemies land 14 to 80 per hit at these stages, so this sits inside their range. Scale the projectile's spawn damage so the cap holds after the vanilla hostile-projectile rule (the player takes twice the projectile's damage, in every difficulty), as `BandStats.Hit` already does.
3. Sustained dps per levy of roughly 15 / 40 / 50 / 50 at stages 0 / 1 / 3 / 5 (the median ordinary Expert enemy), which at 1.3 landed hits per second means a landed hit of 12 to 40. Because attackers share the i-frames, a band of three needs no extra attack-token rule for damage; keep it for pressure and readability.
4. Defense: the NPC rule is damage minus half the defense, so hostile levy defense is only a speed bump against fast weapons and irrelevant against slow strong ones (see the armor table). A stage defense line near the Levy one (8 / 14 / 34 / 46) is enough; real armor (up to 55 to 75) makes Megashark, Crystal Storm and Nettle Burst take 15 to 30 seconds per 1,000 life or never finish. Hostile levies need no per-hit immunity of their own beyond vanilla (it does nothing against bullets and arrows, which die on hit).
5. PvP is not a good model: PvP counts the victim's defense at half, ignores the difficulty, uses 8-tick i-frames and has no doubling for projectiles. It matches a Classic-style fight, not an Expert enemy. Use the hostile-projectile rule instead.

## What is measured and what is computed

| Part | How |
|---|---|
| Levy fights (tables 1 to 4) | **Measured.** Real Levy soldiers with the real weapon, ammo and Levy aim fire at an invisible proxy NPC the size of the player that strafes on the aim lab's path (6 s horizontal, 3 s vertical, about 3 px per tick), 16 tiles away. Every hit the proxy takes becomes a real `Player.Hurt` on the stand-in commander, who wears the stage kit through the real equip pass (so defense, endurance and i-frames come from the game). A hit by a projectile uses the vanilla hostile-projectile rule; a melee strike uses the NPC-contact rule. Life is held at 1,000,000 and the death time is the moment the damage taken reaches the stage's life. 10 seeds per cell (8 for max life, no-i-frames and Cross Necklace). Damage is what Levy fires (item and ammo damage times Levy's grade 0.8 and class bonuses, column "levy shot dmg"), then x2 for the hostile-projectile rule. |
| Enemy fights (table 5) | **Measured.** Real vanilla enemies with their own AI attack the same strafing stand-in on the arena floor; contact runs the real `Player.Update_NPCCollision`, projectiles the replayed `Projectile.Damage` lines. 8 seeds. Some AI is patchy (the Necromancer never landed a bolt, the Pixie and Fire Imp often keep their distance), so low rows are lower bounds. |
| Defense/immunity tables (6) | **Real damage functions, scripted timeline.** Real `NPC.CalculateHitInfo` and `NPC.StrikeNPC` (defense rule, crit, damage variance) on a real NPC with set defense, fed by the real item and ammo damage and use times; the timeline (one shot every use time, all shots hit a standing target, 100 trials of 30 s) and the three immunity models are my scripting. No aim error. |
| PvP table (7) | **Real `Player.Hurt`** with `pvp: true` and with the hostile-projectile and contact inputs, same scripted timeline (all shots hit, 40 trials of 30 s), against the stage kit. |
| Rules quoted from source | Read from the decompiled game (`Projectile.cs` lines 13414 to 13441 and 11560+, `Player.cs` line 34882, `Player.Update_NPCCollision`, `NPC.GetAttackDamage_*`). |

Not run: Master, Calamity (the vanilla world only, so no Calamity extra life items), the "spawn at half damage" arm (dropped for time; the hit rate is the same, the per-hit damage is linear in the spawn damage apart from defense).

## The rules, from the game's code

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Player immunity after a hit | 40 ticks; 20 if the hit did exactly 1 damage; 80 and 40 with Cross Necklace (`longInvince`). **PvP hit: 8 ticks**, whatever the gear. |
| Defense effectiveness, hits from NPCs and their projectiles | damage minus defense x 0.5 Classic, **x 0.75 Expert**, x 1.0 Master (`Player.DefenseEffectiveness`, measured: 40 damage vs 17 defense gives 31 / 27 / 23). |
| Defense effectiveness, PvP | **x 0.5 in every mode** (measured: the same per-hit damage in Classic, Expert and Master). |
| Hostile projectile hitting a player | `Main.DamageVar(projectile.damage) * 2`, then the defense rule. The doubling is the same in every difficulty; the difficulty enters through the damage the NPC gives the projectile when it fires it (`GetAttackDamage_ForProjectiles`). On a dedicated server the game never runs this for a player-less slot (the client does), so the lab replays it. |
| NPC contact | `Main.DamageVar(npc.damage)`, then the defense rule; `npc.damage` already carries the Expert x2. |
| Damage variance | about plus or minus 15% (`DamageVar`). |
| NPC taking a hit | damage minus defense x 0.5 (all modes). Immunity: a projectile that pierces gives the NPC 10 ticks of immunity to its owner (or its own local cooldown); a projectile that dies on hit (bullets, arrows, most bolts) gives none; a melee swing hits once per swing. So the "vanilla NPC" model equals "no immunity" for every weapon here. |

Reading it: against a lightly armored victim PvP and the hostile-projectile rule give the same order of damage (Phoenix Blaster 103 against 72 per second, Terra Blade 169 against 121). For light bullets the hostile rule hits harder per shot (Megashark at defense 55 in Expert: 6.2 per hit in PvP, 21.6 hostile) because the doubling beats PvP's half-defense only partly, but the 8-tick PvP frames let fast weapons land far more often, so dps ends up close (Megashark 27 PvP against 31 hostile; Clockwork Assault Rifle 29 against 22). In Master the hostile rule counts defense in full and falls clearly below PvP for light weapons (Megashark 14.5 against 26.7). A player weapon fired by a player therefore does not annihilate a player more than the same weapon fired as a hostile projectile in Expert. What differs is the ordinary enemy: its per-hit damage comes from its own table and is 5 to 20 times smaller than a late-stage weapon's (Zombie 15, Pixie 66, Skeleton Sniper 134, against Sniper Rifle 190 to 290).
