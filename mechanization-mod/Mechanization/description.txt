Mechanization — the machine arm of the levy.

Where Levy raises soldiers, Mechanization fields drones, turrets and vehicles that
fight alongside them. Requires the Levy mod.

v0.2.0: the automatic turret now survives a world reload and arms itself from the
best spare ranged weapon in a nearby Levy armory, firing that weapon's real rounds
(falling back to a plain round when no armory is in reach). Still destructible, with
its own healthbar.
