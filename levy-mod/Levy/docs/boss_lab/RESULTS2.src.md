## 2. Fear on vs off (FearMultiplier=0), same seeds
Paired on the seeds that exist in both arms (12 seeds where the main cell has 12, else 6). Arm "fear on" is the main table above.

@@FEAR@@

Observed:
- Fear matters a lot in the cells the lab flagged: **Eye Rev 2/12 -> 7/12 wins** (kill time 162 -> 102 s, deaths 9.4 -> 7.9), **EoW Expert 4/12 -> 8/12** (139 -> 57 s), **Brain Rev 0/6 -> 3/6**, **Queen Bee Expert 2/12 -> 7/12**, QB Rev 8/12 -> 9/12 (deaths 3.2 -> 6.7: fear-off kills faster, with more casualties), QB Death 9/12 -> 11/12.
- Boss damage/s roughly doubles to triples with fear off (EoW Exp 174 -> 336, Brain Rev 1.6 -> 28.8, QB Exp 23 -> 72). Fear suppresses levy firing, not just levy morale.
- Desert Scourge (Expert and Rev) is unchanged: 0/6 both ways, 11/11 dead. Worm contact kills the levies faster than fear can matter (first death 6-9 s).
- EoW Rev: 0/6 both ways, deaths unchanged at 13; fear-off only doubles boss damage/s. Fear does not rescue Rev EoW.
- First-death time falls with fear off (Eye Rev 68 -> 34 s, QB Rev 102 -> 28 s) because levies stay in the fight instead of backing off. Fewer total deaths in the cells where the fight ends sooner, but not fewer early deaths.
- Interpretation (inferred): with fear on, levies disengage and the fight lengthens; in long Rev fights the boss gets many more attack cycles on a thinning army. That is why fear-on loses the Eye/Brain/EoW cells that fear-off wins at roughly 40-60% rates.

## 5. The Queen Bee and Eye questions
Queen Bee, Expert vs Rev/Death (lead's question). Hypotheses tested:
1. **Levy Rage/Adrenaline soaking her charges: ruled out.** `revoff` (Revengeance on, levy rippers off, 6 seeds): 1/0/5/0, deaths 3.7 (1-5 each) vs Rev with rippers 3.2. Without Rage/Adrenaline she still barely kills anyone, and the fights are slower (5 timeouts) because the army hits less hard. Rippers change speed, not the death count.
2. **Different jungle/zone per mode: ruled out.** The same `jungle=1` arena for all modes. `enragedSec` equals the full fight in Rev/Death whether `jungle=1` or not (dbg seed 1, jungle=0: enraged 213 s, jungle=1: 291 s); `bossMax` is 8568 in all Rev/Death rows regardless of jungle. The enrage flag is not a zone effect.
3. **Calamity's Rev QB AI behaves differently (supported, observed).** The Expert fight is the vanilla QB: bees `addsPeak` 10.8 (6-17), the dbg trace shows `ai[0]=2` (15-30% of the 30-tick samples) and clusters of deaths (15 -> 4 alive within 15 s). In Rev/Death `addsPeak` is 1, `ai[0]` never reaches 2, and deaths are 2.6-3.7. Contact damage per second on the army: Expert ~36 dmg/s (4,700 over ~130 s), Rev ~14 dmg/s (4,000 over ~290 s). QB's HP is higher in Rev (8568 vs 4760, defense 14 vs 8), so Rev lasts 2x longer yet kills far fewer levies. Damage source in all modes is QB body contact (`contact:Queen Bee` 3,000-4,800 per fight; bees 10-114; stingers 40-340). Cause inferred: Calamity replaces the vanilla QB AI in Rev/Death (no bee swarm, fewer/slower dashes); I did not read the Calamity source for this round.
4. **Seed luck: ruled out.** 12 seeds per arm: Expert 2 wins, 13.6 dead; Rev 8 wins, 3.2 dead; Death 9 wins, 3.0 dead.
- Verdict: it is the AI, not the levies, not the zone. Eve will find vanilla-AI Expert QB (the dedicated server runs vanilla Expert AI, Calamity overrides only in Rev/Death) deadlier than Calamity Rev QB. If Eve wants Expert QB softened, the lever is the bee/dash pressure in vanilla Expert, not Rev tuning.

Eye of Cthulhu, Rev (lead's smaller question): boss target P is 0%, adds target levies 100%, and the Eye still wins 10/12. `revoff` (rippers off) is 0/6 wins, 11/11 dead in every fight: the levy Rage/Adrenaline is not the cause of the losses either, and turning it off is worse (kill times unaffected, deaths 11 vs 9.4). Fear matters (7/12 wins fear-off). Conclusion (observed): real lethality of the Calamity Rev Eye against the 11 real-count levies, with fear compounding it. The count cliff from lab 1 section 2 still applies (11 levies sit on it).
