# Paladin's Shield lab: Expert Eater of Worlds, 45 levies

Harness: calrev + Lab3, new token `pl=shield|none|tankA|tankB` (`Common/Testing/CombatHarness.Paladin.cs`), arena a3, `LEVY_CR_SP=1`, mode `exp` (Expert, plain Eater of Worlds), cap 5 min, seeds 1-4, 4 fights per arm. Weapons come from `Lab3Roll(seed)` and are identical across arms; spawn slots are identical; only the shield/tank kit differs. Every levy: no armor, 400 max HP (15 crystals), no potions, no accessories except the arm's. New counters in `TeamPlayer`: `PaladinSoaks`, `PaladinSoakDone`, `PaladinHits`, `PaladinHitDmg`, `PaladinNested`.

Arms: **1a** 45 levies, all with a Paladin's Shield. **1b** the same 45, none. **2a** tank (slot 43, at the worm's side) with Solar Flare armor, Worm Scarf, Frozen Turtle Shell, Ankh/Obsidian/Cobalt Shield, Endurance + Ironskin (measured defense 98, damage reduction 30%), two shield holders either side, 42 plain levies. **2b** the same slots without the tank kit (the two flankers keep their shields).

## Answers
1. **Shields do not make the group sturdier here; they push the first death later and cost more total HP.** First death comes about 6 s later (19 vs 13 s), but deaths are 15 vs 12 and total damage taken is 40% higher. N=4 per arm and the sim is chaotic (1b seed 2 took 95 s with zero deaths), so deaths and kill time are not separable; the damage numbers are not noise. Cause (from the counters): the shielded hits' own damage was 3,442 in total, the shares struck on payers 4,444, i.e. 1.3x the hit, not the intended third. `TeamPlayer.OnHurt` and `SoldierNPC.SetsOnHurt` use `total = max(holders, hit/3)`: with ~18 holders in range every hit becomes 18+ one-damage shares. With no armor each share costs a full 1 HP, so a 13-damage hit also costs about 18 HP elsewhere. Armor would cut that (each share is reduced by the payer's defense, min 1), which is the unarmored worst case. Flagging, not changing: tenet 1 says no cap on the toy, but the "total stays a third" comment is not true above ~3 holders.
2. **Yes, in this setup the stacked tank helps a lot.** Deaths 1 vs 14, damage taken 1,411 vs 2,504, no tank death, the tank took about 112 damage in about 90 hits (defense 98 means a hit does about 1). The worm keeps biting the front-slot tank, so the rest of the group is hit less. Kill time was 7 s slower (44.6 vs 37.2): the worm also spends its time on an unkillable target and fewer levies are alive near it. The two flankers paid little (about 80 life for the whole fight).
3. **No cascade.** `TakeSoak` sets `_soaking`, calls `IncomingSetScale(false)`, which clears `_hitPaladins`/`_hitPaladinPlayer` and skips the Paladin scan, so the payer's own `SetsOnHurt` finds zero holders. A new counter `PaladinNested` (a share whose own wound found holders) read **0 in all 16 fights**, across 4,444+ shares in the 45-shield arm. Exactly one round per hit: shares struck equal the sum of holders per hit (about 18 per hit), never a second split. The only floor is the 1-damage minimum per holder noted above, and it applies once per original hit.
4. **Yes, a transfer counts as a hit for Adrenaline.** Calamity's `LoseAdrenalineOnHurt` (CalPlayer/CalamityPlayerHitHurt.cs) returns early only for `hurtInfo.Damage <= 0` ("being hit for zero from Paladin's Shield damage share has no effect") or while Adrenaline Mode is burning. Any share of 1 or more goes through `Player.Hurt` and is a normal hit: Adrenaline pauses and falls by 10-100% by size, to zero if the bar was full. Levy matches this: `TakeSoak` ends in `AfterHit(done, ...)`, which calls `Rippers_OnHurt(done)` -> `LevyRippers.LoseOnHurt` (same Calamity method) for any `done > 0`; the victim itself also loses Adrenaline (for a full bar its hit is halved first, then the bar empties). Consequence: in Rev/Death every holder that pays a share loses Adrenaline, and because of the 1-HP-per-holder floor, one hit on a big shield group touches about 18 bars. Not run in the harness (read from source and code only).
