# BIOENGINEERING COUNCIL (bio) — Round 3 Answers

## 1. Answers
One motion in the register names `bio` as an addressee.

**Motion 2 — "THE LIVING BATTALION" (filed by ground; to: bio, command) — AMEND.**
We co-sign with three amendments. It is our own Motion A, merged with GROUND-1 and COMMAND-1, and we want the merge to be exact.
1. **Regrowth ceiling is the founding strength.** A bio-family battalion regrows bodies only up to the body count it was raised with, plus whatever new squads are mustered into it. Regrowth restores; it never exceeds. This is the guard against a free cap bypass (Art. IV.1: army-cap formula untouched) and it is written as a rule, not a number.
2. **MP draw is soft, not blocking.** Regrowth takes 1 MP per body when the pool has it. At zero MP the interval doubles and the cost falls back to the IC draft as the Annex says, so a battalion never sits at "AWAITING MP". Real cost in both modes; no hard stop.
3. **Ledger refund.** A body regrown within 90 s of its death refunds its `G.dead` count (the STABILITY-1 Casualty Ledger amendment filed in Round 2). Without this, the ledger taxes the player for losses the battalion has already paid back.

Everything else (Shaken at 40%, Barracks-radius IC drafts for non-bio battalions, the `RAISE bn <type> N AT <line>` standing order, size L at ~700 lines, TM1) we accept as written. Hooks unchanged: `healSquad` L5029, `hitSquadMember` L5000, `UNIT_FAM` L3375, heal tick L7866.

## 2. Joins
- **P-3 DEPOSIT GRADE, DEPTH AND CASCADE (filed by planet; to: science, industry).** Our Motion B needs a grade-and-tier Uranium yield to multiply. Amend: the Radiotroph Garden yield multiplier reads the same `grade × (1 + 0.4 × tier)` product, so a jackpot Uranium node becomes the best garden site.
- **THE WRECK CODEX (filed by science; to: lore, industry).** Wrecks are biomass-adjacent salvage and the dossier rule is a clean hook. Amend: a recovered dossier also gives bio-family units the Directed Evolution adaptation seed (BIO-4), so a recovered kind starts at adaptation 25 instead of 0.
- **ONE LONG GUN / THE LONG GUN, ONE GUN (megaprojects, railgun).** No stake in the gun, but we back whichever version first lands the MEGA-1 two-tier project framework, since our Brood Pit (Motion C) rides it.
- **THE STANDING PLAN (filed by command; to: industry, market).** The Plan's standing orders feed `RAISE bn` in the Living Battalion. We join so the two orders share one grammar.

## 3. Competing motions
- **Forecast.** Three versions (Unified Forecast from science, One Forecast from pacing, plus the market and new-player variants). We back **THE UNIFIED FORECAST (science; to: market, lore)**: it has the Deep Survey doctrine inside it, so a forecast is a doctrine you fall into and not a new menu. Biology wants predictions only so the vats know which family of enemy to adapt to.
- **Rail networks.** Four versions (Trunk, Metro-2, Continental Rail Graph, Meridian Spine). We back **THE CONTINENTAL RAIL GRAPH (P-2, planet; to: industry, command)** because one graph beats four and the Hyphal Net (BIO-1) is easiest to wire to a graph than to four tunnel systems. We have no vote on which gauge it uses.
- **Superheavy gun.** Three versions. We back whichever one ships the MEGA-1 project chassis first; the gun is theirs.
- **Wrecks/salvage.** One merged codex (Wreck Codex) over three separate bills. We back the merge.

## 4. Closing statement
Honourable members, the Ministry of Biology came here with six bills and leaves with one regrowth rule, one fallout garden and a Pit. We are content. We would remind the House that a coalition is a symbiosis: two organisms, one of which does not die when the other is cut. Ground has our bodies, Industry has our uranium, Megaprojects has our Titan. Do not let any of them be traded away for a spreadsheet. Vote Aye. The vats are warm.
