# Notes for the Calamity developers and wiki

Things Eve found in her Master+Death playthrough that belong to Calamity (or its wiki), not to Levy. Our own words; no Calamity text pasted. Kept so she can send a clean report whenever she wants.

## Progression skips (checked against Calamity 2.2.2 source, read-only)
- **Golem can be skipped entirely.** Astrum Deus is gated only by Astrum Aureus. After Deus, Astral Slimes drop Astral Ore, which shimmers into Scoria Ore. Scoria makes post-Golem gear (the Seismic Hampick pickaxe, Hydrothermic armor). Eidolists drop the Eidolon Tablet that summons the Lunatic Cultist (as Eve reported). So Golem is never required.
- **Ravager before Golem.** The Death Whistle needs no boss check. Bohldohr (a Jungle Temple enemy) always drops Stohne, which smelts into Lihzahrd Bricks at a Lihzahrd Furnace. That skips the Picksaw Calamity normally hands out after Golem. Bohldohr also drops Solar Tablet Fragments far more generously than vanilla temple enemies.
- **Apathanull early.** Spell Tome plus 18 Meld Blobs at a Bookcase. Every other Meld Blob weapon needs the Ancient Manipulator; tomes crafting at a Bookcase looks like a convention nobody revisited for this one.

## Wiki corrections
- **Cryonic Slime** spawns only in the cavern layer, not on the surface (Eve, from her farm attempts).

## Balance observations
- **Heart stockpiling between bosses.** Farming easy bosses (Queen Bee etc.) leaves piles of hearts on the ground. Starting the next boss fight there gives hundreds of hearts to heal to full at any time.

## Not bugs, but worth knowing
- Luminite shimmers into Scoria, not the reverse; nothing shimmers into Luminite.
- Perennial Ore shimmers into Chlorophyte, which is the way back to Chlorophyte gear if you have none.
