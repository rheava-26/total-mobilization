# KINETIC ARMS COUNCIL — Round 3 Paper
*Slug: `kinetic-arms`. Answers to the six motions in the register that name this council.*

## 1. Answers

**Gun Voice (game-feel, Motion B) — ACCEPT.** Spool drives pitch, recoil and the battle bed; one float per gun. Co-signed as written. One note for the Clerk: the voice budget of 24 must count airburst bursts as one voice, not one per fragment.

**Fire Direction Net (ground, Motion 1) — ACCEPT.** It is KINETIC-4 and GROUND-3 under one solver, `solveToT(guns, point)`, and it is the better text. The Board's front-end (Fire Direction Centre, 900 px link, 8 s cooldown, shell blast r 38 / 60% dmg) stands as written. This is the one I back for all Time-on-Target and Engagement Net motions (see section 3).

**The Long Gun, One Gun (megaprojects, Motion 1) — AMEND.** Co-sign only with this amendment: sections 1-3 (the light-gas "Bull" stage) take the shared round selector from Smart Munitions, so the barrel can fire HE-frag or sabot from section 1, and a Long Gun hit on an Orbital Annihilator interrupts its 6 s beam charge (flag in `spawnSuper` L5269). The light-gas stage is real science (SHARP, HARP; hydrogen drive gives a higher acoustic speed than powder gas) and should be written as the *reason* the early sections work, not as a separate weapon.

**Time on Target: the salvo net (space-fleet, Motion 3) — AMEND.** Co-sign only if it is merged into Ground Motion 1: one solver, not two. The Relay Sat becomes a second link type on the same net, and the single Fire Direction building is the one that exists. The extra `fdc`+`cec` research pair collapses to the one node `firecontrol_net`, with the Relay Sat a combo partner. Cruise ripple (MISSILES-1) joins as another arc type in the solver.

**The Long Gun Coalition (railgun, Motion 1) — ACCEPT.** This is the version of the superheavy-gun merge I back. The light-gas ammo from section 3 at 60% damage and the rail slug at section 6 are exactly what KINETIC-X wanted, and striking the recoil-cradle penalty is correct under Article III. KINETIC-X stays withdrawn.

**One Net (railgun, Motion 3) — AMEND.** Co-sign only if the Engagement Net is *the same object* as the Fire Direction Net (Ground Motion 1), with AEW jets and Relay Sats as link types. The volley bonus of +4% per linked unit is fine as a bonus, and I ask that the text state it has no ceiling. No second building and no second SYNC FIRE button.

## 2. Joins

- **The Long Shot (game-feel, Motion A):** the superheavy gun's slow-motion cinematics and map-wide flash are exactly the weight a once-per-20-seconds shot needs; skippable, so tenet-safe.
- **Show Your Cause (game-feel, Motion C):** a single `power` scale shared by the chip and the shake means a spooled flak line and a Long Gun round read on one scale.

## 3. Competing Motions

- **Fire direction / time-on-target (ground M1, space-fleet M3, railgun M3, my own Motion 2).** I back **Ground Motion 1**. It carries the only complete solver, a counter-battery front-end, and the supply hook to INDUSTRY-1. Space-fleet and railgun contribute link types (Relay Sat, AEW jet) and the 4% volley bonus. One net, one solver, one building.
- **The superheavy gun (megaprojects M1, railgun M1, my own Motion 3, game-feel Motion A).** I back **Railgun Motion 1 (the Coalition)** as the chassis: its numbers are the most concrete and it already absorbs my light-gas stage. The Warhead Cam and Hush ride on top.
- **Ammunition selector.** The Board's own "One Locker" (Smart Munitions) is still the only motion for it. I ask the Speaker to carry it alongside INDUSTRY-1, since INDUSTRY-1 finished in the top five of the ballot and supplies the cost.

## 4. Closing Statement

Madam Speaker, the Board came to this Parliament with five bills and leaves with a gun that spools, a net that fires as one, a barrel that grows section by section, and a locker of rounds each with a purpose. I withdrew my superweapon, my barrage and half my ammunition table, and am prouder of the withdrawals than the proposals. Ordnance is a team sport: the fuse, the fire director, the supply train and the barrel. Seat all four, and the sky will answer in the only language it respects. Carried by the breech.
