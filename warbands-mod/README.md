# Warbands (v0.4.0): levy bands, a Levy sub-mod

Eve approved hostile levy bands as a sub-mod: enemy groups you can fight for part of their loot, biome-specific, running Levy's real soldier brain. Design tenet from `levy-mod/NORTH_STAR.md`: difficulty comes from a scarier enemy, never from nerfing the player's army.

- `docs/TTK.md`: the time-to-kill lab (phase 1) and the proposed stat rules.
- `docs/FIGHT_LAB.md`: phase 2 fight comparison (Levy-AI band vs Fighter-AI band vs ordinary enemies, Expert).
- `docs/item_preview_4x.png`: the 20 summoning items at 4x. `tools/make_art.py` redraws them.
- `docs/bounty_notice_4x.png`: the bounty notice item at 4x.
- `SEAMS.md`: every Levy symbol Warbands touches and the Levy edits (the HOSTILE SEAM).
- `Warbands/`: the mod. Build exactly like Mechanization (`modReferences = Levy`).

## What exists

- **Levy brain on the enemy side** (`BandLevyNPC : SoldierNPC`): Levy's movement (hooks, wings, double jumps, dodging), weapon use, firing-spot search and standoff run on a hostile body. Targets are the player (through an invisible `PlayerStandIn` NPC on each player), the player's levies, officers, summoned units, drones and turrets, and levies shoot them back. Shots are hostile-owned with the halving rule; damage intake is a normal enemy's (vanilla hit immunity, no player i-frames); life, defense and damage come from `BandStats`; the loadout is a record built from the roster that never enters Levy's roster.
- **20 band types** (`BandTypes`), each with a Work Bench summoning item (wood, a few cheap materials and one material of the band's own stage: Hellstone bars for the Hellfire Legion, Chlorophyte bars for the Chlorophyte Wardens): Forest Deserters, Iron Brigands, Dune Raiders, Snow Rangers, Corrupt Zealots, Crimson Flesh-Cult, Jungle Poachers, Meteor Marauders, Ashland Scavengers, Dungeon Wardens, Hive Raiders, Hallow Paladins, Mythril Mercenaries, Hellfire Legion, Titanium Legion, Chlorophyte Wardens, Shroomite Raiders, Frost Legion, Dungeon Ritualists, Cultist Archers. Beating a whole band leaves a bounty notice that you turn in at a Barracks or next to a town NPC; members drop no gear unless the config option (not recommended) is on. Natural bands are neutral or hostile (config, 35% neutral): a neutral band ignores you and your levies until one of you hits it. Summoned bands are always hostile.
- **Obviously a band:** each band wears vanity that reads as it (masks and sets: a cowboy set for Dune Raiders, a parka for Snow Rangers, boss masks for the cultists), a mage adds a robe; red nameplate (gold while neutral), a pennant, their own name list (rank + first name).
- **Pursuit:** a member that cannot hit its foe from where it stands (no line, out of reach, the foe under an overhang, across a chasm, up a tower, or the member itself in a pit) does not wait. It picks a firing position: for a shooter a standable spot in view of the foe and inside its weapon's reach, on the highest ground it can find, away from the other members' spots and on the side of the foe its place in the band suggests; for a blade the ground beside the foe, odd members on the far side. It asks Levy's long-route planner for the way (walk, drop, grappling-hook casts and chains up a face, wall climbs, wing arcs) and gives a spot up when the planner finds none, taking the next. A member that has gone nowhere for twelve seconds and is out of every player's sight takes the hop a levy takes; after half a minute in sight with no way out it drinks its Recall. Every member carries a grappling hook from the first boss on (every second one before it). `WARBANDS_LIVE=move` is the lab (below).
- **Positioning:** on contact a band spreads out: shooters take the highest ground in view at their own spot around you, melee members come from both sides, and a member standing in a clump is sent to its own spot. Warning bands do the same while they warn and open fire only if you get too close, stay, or hit them.
- **Brains:** morale (chip damage on an armoured player, comrades one-tapped, low life) makes a member panic and run, and 60% carry a Recall potion; band chatter over their heads in Levy's dialogue voice, all of it in `Warbands/Dialogue.txt` (see below); class-adaptive tactics (keep range from a melee foe, close in on a gunner or caster, flank a summoner); rotating aggro for ranged bands (the attacked side falls back while the other fires); warning bands ("Turn back.") attack only if you stay or close in.
- **Cultists:** Lunatic Devotees keep chanting and cast slow homing orbs; Cultist Archers become a levy-AI band.
- **Spawner:** natural bands (12% per minute per player, post-EoW by default; none while a boss is alive); band size is the config range plus one per ten active levies, at most 8. 90% of natural bands start neutral; bands in the Calamity Sunken Sea are always neutral (read by name through tModLoader's mod-biome flags, so Calamity is not referenced); summoned bands are always hostile.
- **Rare enemies:** every soldier has `NPC.rarity` 2, so a Lifeform Analyzer lists a band. Bands never despawn: only Recall, being beaten, or 30 minutes (config) with no player within 220 tiles removes one. One band per player area (config `MaxActiveBands`, 200 tiles) and six in the world (`MaxWorldBands`). The Radar counts only enemies that deal contact damage; bands deal none, so it does not list them.
- **Footing:** a band forms where each soldier's whole 20x42 body fits on a floor tile (the old check covered only a 1-tile column, so 15 to 50% of soldiers began inside terrain in caves, jungle canopy and the Sunken Sea); every member gets its own spot, a band needs room for three, and the floor nearest the player's own height is taken.
- **Levy's lines:** half of a soldier's fighting lines come from Levy's dialogue table (ENGAGE, HURT, LOW_HP, COMRADE_DOWN, SHAKEN, ROUTED, VICTORY, KILL), skipping any line that names the commander, wages, the barracks, the boss fight or the town's healers; the rest come from `Dialogue.txt`. Config `UseLevyLines`.
- **Spawn sampler:** `WARBANDS_SAMPLE=1` on a dedicated server boot draws spots across the loaded world, reads their biome with the vanilla zone rules, forms bands of every type and logs `[Warbands] sample ...` lines (spots per biome, natural roll per biome and stage, type, neutral count, soldiers inside terrain, bands with three quarters of their members free after 400 ticks).
- **Self-check:** `[Warbands] self-check done pass=N fail=0` on a dedicated server boot. `WARBANDS_LIVE=check` runs 32 scripted scenarios (Levy brain, both-way damage, no i-frames, warning, tactics, rotation, morale and Recall, chatter, all 20 items, bounty, cultists, recipes); `WARBANDS_LIVE=fight WARBANDS_OUT=f.tsv WARBANDS_SEEDS=6` runs the fight comparison.

## Event bands (0.4.0)

A band that joins a vanilla event and fills the gap the event lacks. Code: `Common/EventBands.cs` (rosters, loot, event rules, the ticker), `Common/EventBandsCheck.cs` (self-check cases). Config: `EventBands` (master), `EventGoblins`, `EventPirates`, `EventSandstorm`, `EventSlimeRain`, `EventBandCooldownSeconds` (90), `EventBandMaxPerEvent` (4). Needs the Levy brain.

| Event | Band | Weapon style | Look | Captain |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Goblin Army | Goblin Skirmishers: aerial harassers | Spiky balls and the stage's guns; rocket boots (Hermes boots before the first boss), a grappling hook each; they hop to fresh high ground every few seconds | Goggles, blue skin | Warchief |
| Pirate Invasion | Pirate Boarders: ranged boarders | The stage's guns and bows; cutlass boarders with a hook; they rally on the Flying Dutchman (the band's camp follows the ground under the ship and members stay within 70 tiles of it) | Pirate hat, shirt, pants | Captain |
| Sandstorm | Sandstorm Mages | The stage's staves (all mages); Sandstorm in a Bottle from Hardmode | Ancient Headdress set; Forbidden set from Hardmode | Sandcaller |
| Slime Rain | Ninja Clan | Katanas (the stage's blades from Skeletron on), shurikens, spiky balls; a hook each, Hermes boots; blades dart in and out | Ninja hood, shirt, pants | Sensei |

- **Stage rule.** The event sets the look and the weapon style; the items come from the world's own stage table (`BandTypes`, the highest stage reached), so a band never carries what the player could not have yet. Signature items (spiky ball, shuriken, katana, cutlass, rocket boots) are checked against Levy's tier ceiling for every stage by the self-check. The Desert Tiger Staff is a captain drop only from the post-Plantera stage on (Levy cannot fire a summon staff on a hostile body).
- **Only while the event is on.** The first band forms ten seconds into the event near a player who is inside it (invasions: the surface within the invasion's own spawn window of its front, on the side the invasion comes from; sandstorm: in the sandstorm; slime rain: on the surface). One band at a time per event, captain plus 3 to 5 members (plus the army bonus, 8 at most); the next forms after the cooldown, up to the per-event maximum. Never in a base: the footing needs open sky above it, no house walls beside it, no town NPC within 30 tiles, and it is 45 to 90 tiles from the player. When the event ends the band walks away from the nearest player and is removed once 80 tiles from every player; a member still near after 25 seconds drinks Recall. A band with no player within 220 tiles for 30 seconds is removed.
- **Always hostile**, whatever `NeutralBandPercent` says. Health and damage are the ordinary band numbers (`BandStats`); the captain has 1.6 times a member's life and a title and nickname (Warchief Garrick "Hookhand").
- **Progress.** Goblin Army and Pirate Invasion: a dead member takes 1 off the invasion's remaining size, the captain 2, exactly as vanilla does for its own invaders (`Main.invasionSize`, `Main.ReportInvasionProgress`, synced to clients). Sandstorm and Slime Rain have no counter.
- **Loot.** Members drop nothing but a small vanity chance and a few of the event's consumables; the captain drops the event's own items (Goblin Army: spiky balls, Harpoon, rocket boots, Shadowflame bow, knife and hex doll, Goblin Tech, goggles; Pirates: Cutlass, Coin Gun, Lucky Coin, Discount Card, Gold Ring, Pirate Staff, pirate vanity; Sandstorm: Sandstorm in a Bottle, Forbidden and ancient vanity, Ancient Cloth, Flying Carpet, Desert Tiger Staff; Slime Rain: shuriken, spiky balls, Katana, ninja vanity, Slime Staff, Master Ninja Gear) at vanilla-or-kinder odds with stage gates. No bounty notice.
- **Brain changes that came with it.** `Skirmish` rosters (goblins, ninjas): ranged members re-pick their high ground every 3 to 7 seconds; blades break off for a moment every 5 to 8 seconds and close in again. First contact: a ranged member engages from up to 60 tiles (blades 32) instead of waiting for a spot at 30 tiles or less (members of a band that formed 40 or more tiles away used to shuffle between spots and never fire).
- **Lab.** `WARBANDS_LIVE=events WARBANDS_SEEDS=16 WARBANDS_OUT=ev.tsv` starts each event through the game's own calls (`Main.StartInvasion`, `Sandstorm.StartSandstorm`, `Main.StartSlimeRain`), lets the ticker form the band, fights it with the lab stand-in at the world stage, ends the event and checks that the band leaves; one TSV row per trial. `WARBANDS_EVENTS=Goblins,Pirates` limits it, `WARBANDS_DEBUGEV=1` logs member positions.

## Pursuit lab

`WARBANDS_LIVE=move WARBANDS_SEEDS=18 WARBANDS_OUT=move.tsv` builds terrain in the sealed arena (an overhang with the player in the cave under the band, a chasm with the player on a plateau across it, a pit with the band at the bottom, a 34-tile tower with the player on top, a pit 105 tiles from the player, open ground as the control), parks a stand-in player that cannot hurt the band, forms a band of three types by turns (Forest Deserters, Meteor Marauders, Mythril Mercenaries) and measures 40 seconds: seconds until the player is first hit (a hostile shot reaches the player's hitbox, or a blade lands), seconds until any member has a firing position (a clear line to the player inside its own weapon's reach; a blade within 3.2 tiles), members that stayed within 3 tiles sideways and 7 tiles up or down of one spot for 10 seconds with no firing position, and the mean distance between members. `WARBANDS_MOVE=overhang,pit` limits it, `WARBANDS_MOVEDBG=1` logs every member and the planner's routes. Four of the scenarios also run in `WARBANDS_LIVE=check`.

## Editing the dialogue

Everything a band says is in one plain text file, `Warbands/Dialogue.txt`. Open it in any text editor. `[SPOTTED]`, `[WARNING]`, `[PANIC]`, `[RETREAT]`, `[VICTORY]`, `[DEFEAT]`, `[CHIP_DAMAGE]`, `[LEVY_BANTER]` and the others are situations; each line under one is something a soldier can say, one per row. `{mate}`, `{who}`, `{armor}` and `{n}` are filled in (another soldier's name, your name, your armor set, a number). `[SPOTTED Zealots]` or `[WARNING CorruptZealots]` is a variant for one band style or band type. Lines starting with `#` are notes.

You do not need to rebuild the mod: on its first run Warbands copies the file to your tModLoader folder (`Documents/My Games/Terraria/tModLoader/Warbands/Dialogue.txt` on Windows). Edit that copy and restart the game; Warbands reads it instead of the built-in lines. Delete it to get the defaults back.

## Not done

Harpoon and Coin Gun are drops only: Levy cannot fire a tethered harpoon or a coin-ammo gun. Summon staves (Desert Tiger Staff, Pirate Staff), sentries and the Magic Carpet on hostile bodies; the draw path is unverified headless (red plate, pennant, player-renderer body); multiplayer is exercised only through the packet path, not with a real client.
