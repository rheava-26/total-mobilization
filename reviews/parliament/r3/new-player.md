# NEW PLAYER COUNCIL — Round 3: Coalitions

## 1. Answers

**Show Your Cause (game-feel) — AMEND.**
The shared 0–12 power scale is welcome. Amendments:
1. The chip has two channels. A *refusal* chip is persistent and static (lift, hangar, parts). An *event* chip is transient ("Reaper-P hit: 6"). Only the event channel reads `power`.
2. The chip text never depends on the shake. Safe Mode and `reducedMotion` zero the shake, and the chip, its cause and its power number still show.
3. Chip text follows the player-text style: what, how many, what fixes it, with no ALL-CAPS.

**The Exercise (lore) — AMEND.**
I accept the merge, drill wave, scoring and reveal. I withdrew NEW-PLAYER-6 into LORE-4 in Round 2, so the base is LORE-4.
1. Guided and Heavily Guided modes keep one drill wave, with a one-line banner before it naming the lesson it teaches (Reach Curtain, re-entry window, or landing flank).
2. In those two modes the `killsByType` seeding of `threatWeight` runs at half strength. Oversight, Streamlined and No-Tutorial players get full strength, and the +10% IC applies to all.
3. The adaptation is shown, not secret: the forecast reads "Fleet is adapting to: [what you drilled]" at T-0.
4. Veterans can switch the exercise off in Options and lose nothing else.

**The One Forecast (pacing) — AMEND.**
I co-sponsored the same idea, so this is near-ACCEPT. One amendment: tier 0 (zero observers) must show every class name, the four phase names and the 5 prep beats at a fixed baseline band of ±40%. As written, `sigma/sqrt(1+n_obs)` gives no tier-0 baseline, and a recruit who builds no Radar would see nothing.

**The Wire as Home Front (stability) — AMEND.**
1. The Information Policy default is **Managed**, which is today's numbers unchanged. Free Press and Censored are opt-in.
2. Whatever the policy, the hover receipt always shows the *true* contributions. A policy changes game numbers, never the honesty of the display. Censored's deferred bleed appears on the receipt as its own line.
3. The ticker shows one line at a time, holds each 4 s, and the full history opens on tap or hover.
4. Headlines are plain systems text, 1–2 short sentences.

## 2. Joins

- **STANDING ECONOMY (market, to industry and command).** I join. My Round 2 Motion 3 asked for blocked-line chips on the Plan panel. Please carry "Blocked: needs X" status onto each ledger line.
- **THE NERVE (pacing, to autofleet and lore).** I join. The Fear and Resolve needles belong on the forecast panel as the "alarm" and "morale" readouts, so one panel explains the fleet's mind.

## 3. Competing motions

Three near-identical forecast motions exist: Pacing's THE ONE FORECAST, Science's THE UNIFIED FORECAST, and Psi's THE LISTENING.

- **I back Pacing's THE ONE FORECAST as the base.** It names all four faces (manifest and plan strip, desk bands, prep beats, Wire) and sets one `sigma` rule and one `signals` node.
- Science's "25% of lines wrong at low confidence" should be a **tier-1-and-up** rule only. A tier-0 recruit gets wide bands, not false statements, because a first-hour player who is lied to learns to distrust every readout. Wrong lines then become a real intel decision once the player has bought observers.
- Psi's Listening Chamber is welcome as a later, costly active peek on top of the same surface (stability cap cost is a real cost). It ships third, after the base.

## 4. Closing statement

Mr Speaker, we have asked the recruit to love a sky they cannot read. This house now offers one forecast, one set of receipts, and one honest refusal on every locked button. I accept every motion addressed to me, with small edits that each protect a person on their first evening. Nothing in them caps a toy or hides a number. Give the civilian a dial for every danger and she will build the nation you all want.
