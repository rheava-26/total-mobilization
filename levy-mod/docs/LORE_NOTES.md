**Tags.** CANON = the mod's own text, as reported in the thread (reverify exact wording before relying on it). EVE = Eve's idea. PITCH = the assistant's invention, an offer. UNSURE = source unclear, or it survives only in a compaction summary.

Caveats: the world shows things about a century after 1781, so the preset needs a short map-reading guide (PITCH). It was never confirmed whether Pax Historia accepts a custom map.
- **Empty map, Eve's explanation:** monsters spawn wherever there are no walls, so people live in walled towns and the open land is wilderness. The assistant agreed. (EVE)

## 3. Places and factions in 1781

- **Yharim and Yharon:** a traitor and a reborn phoenix in the underworld beside Azafure, with no state yet. (CANON) PITCH: the execution happens near Azafure, so the city watched him rise from the lava, and people follow an executed hero who comes back riding the last dragon.
- **Permafrost, the Archmage:** early ally. He guides the conquests and later governs the captured cities. Home is the snow biome, where only he and miners lived. (CANON)
- **Azafure:** the oldest and largest city, in the underworld beneath the Abyss on the dungeon side (today's Brimstone Crags). Guns, explosives, smithing, magic, and brimstone exported everywhere. The Brimstone Elemental sleeps beneath it as matron. The gods tried to rule it and burned it, so it backed Yharim first. The obsidian ruins are left from its war with the demons, who are scattered clans with no lord. Calamitas isn't born yet. (CANON) PITCH: godless and guild-run, 100,000 to 300,000 people, existing trade shafts to the surface (so Yharim never had to conquer the caves).
- **Dictator deities:** the future evil biome. A surveillance theocracy, "slain gods" plural, the war's first target. (CANON)
- **Ilmeris:** the whole desert is the Ilmeran Sea; the Sunken Sea is its countryside. A constitutional monarchy under Otonilou, the Electric Goddess, and the God of the Seas. It never expanded and never lost a defence (electrified water, trained sand sharks). Several cities with coral homes in sunlit water. Amidias is its 17th ruler; whether he reigns in 1781 is unstated. It had "brutish" neighbours. (CANON) PITCH: the seabed was always sand; cities stood on a shallow seafloor; the patchy underground desert is drowned trenches; the king is real but limited; the neighbours wanted sea prisms.
- **Vernal Pass:** in the jungle, "the hub" of the planet. Silva is alive, Braelor is her champion, and the Pass links to the Lihzahrd temple. (CANON) Eve's screenshots show a tree island with two unbloomed Giant Plantera Bulbs, plain cabins, and no walls or towers: a farming village armed with copper, iron and boomsticks. The bulbs fit her "they were growing more than one" theory. (EVE)
- **Jungle:** Lihzahrds abandoned by their god (Golem's date unknown), bee villages, hidden Dragonfollies. (CANON) Villages around the underground hives. (EVE)
- **Sulphurous Sea and Abyss:** polluted by Azafure, not yet irradiated. The Abyss is a bare trench: Anahita's home, the last wyrms' hideout, with the Old Duke and the Aquatic Scourge. (CANON)
- **Dungeon:** the Dragon Cult's fortress and occult library, with a dragon-age portal; its ritual keeps the Moon Lord sealed. Yharim calls them "once my friends". (CANON) Early support for Yharon, and its role as neutral ground: PITCH.
- **Sky islands, Chrysadia:** lived under the Storm Dragon; only remnants survive by the present, which the Storm Weaver feeds on. (CANON) Alive but fading, small towns, wyvern riders: PITCH.
- **Forest:** unowned in the text. The hints are dethroned dictators, levelled kingdoms, and a Bandit wanted in seven city-states. (CANON) The god-ruled human heartland, including Yharim's homeland: PITCH.
- **Onyx Kinsmen** (Statis's ninjas, tied to the Slime God): on the surface, after the shinobi-hideout world-generation step. (PITCH)
- **Underground cabins:** an unowned mining frontier. (PITCH) **Blood Moons:** canon training events for soldiers; keep them and drop the summoned moons. (PITCH)
- **Off-map:** Draedon, the Dragon Aerie, the Devourer's dimension.
- **Not yet in 1781:** Calamitas, the Wall of Flesh, Draedon's labs, the Plague, the Astral Infection, Plantera, the overgrown Abyss, the ruined Crags, the Sunken Sea as a cave, the desert as sand.
- **Tech and scale:** about pre-hardmode tech (muskets, bombs, spellbooks); lasers come with Draedon. Millions of people on the continent. Champions are as rare as nukes, and gods drafted heroes into godhood to keep them inside the system, which is why Yharim's refusal frightened them. (PITCH)

## 5. Silva

**Eve: Silva is innocent ("my goat").** The evidence:
- Blossom Flux, the legendary bow, carries her message to Plantera. (CANON) Eve rediscovered it on 11-17.
- A world-generation step blesses a jungle grove. (CANON)
- She blessed the first Primordial Wyrm long before the war. (CANON)
- The Abyss is a "reimprisonment". (CANON)
- Her remains rule out the Devourer as her killer. (UNSURE)
- No line in the text accuses her of anything. Braelor fought Yharim, and to Yharim every god was guilty. (CANON)
- She gives life fruit and crystals; the "Life Fruit detected nearby!" readout appears in her village. (EVE)

**Body (CANON):** she was killed elsewhere, and Yharim disposed of her remains in the Abyss. She never lived there. The wyrms were already hiding there from the purges, so her body landed beside the kin she had protected. Her roots grew through the Abyss floor into the underworld, and Azafure, built beneath it, fed on what dripped down. So she died before Azafure fell. The dark PITCH: Yharim dumped her on top of his best ally. Why she was killed: never stated.

**Eve's theories (EVE):**
- **The darkness is deliberate.** Abyss torch tooltips (thermal, void) speak of cleansing magical darkness from it. Natural darkness wouldn't need cleansing, so Silva darkened the Abyss to shelter the wyrms. The 380,000-health guardians fit a sanctuary meant to be unreachable. The assistant agrees it's a strong clue.
- **She left to protect her own:** she went out to stop Yharim burning her champion's home, and he killed her. (Current favourite, 11-17. Her earlier "timeline options A to D" are lost; UNSURE.)
- **"Shaped in her image":** Yharim's taunt. The Abyss is full of life because of her, not him.
- **The villagers, not Silva, made the chemical weapons:** Plantera's spore gas was the Vernal Pass panicking about Yharim. On 11-15 she joked mid-rage that Silva would have used anthrax, then took it back. Earlier she said Yharim was "projecting" about the village, and that it acted from fear, not pride.
- She was gifted her soul. (lost thread)

**Design:** open. Eve wants something partly human, not the generic leaf-woman. PITCH: her apparent age shifts with the season; a heart motif in life-crystal red; a body that is an ecosystem.

**The Plague, Draedon and Yharim:** the Plague ends their partnership. (CANON) Plaguebringer fires biological payloads ("hive nukes") from a mind-controlled bee machine, so of course Yharim was furious. (EVE) Draedon builds superweapons for whoever pays and acts without consent. (PITCH/EVE) The lost thread said Yharim freed the Plague specimen. (UNSURE, verify)
