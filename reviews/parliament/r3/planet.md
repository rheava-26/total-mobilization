# PLANET & TERRAIN COUNCIL — Round 3: Coalitions (`planet`)

## 1. Answers

**The Trunk, Motion B (command, to industry and planet) — ACCEPT.**
Co-signed as written, provided the one-graph rule in section 3 is the merged version. Numbers: hubs link within 1,000 px, upkeep 1.5 IC/s per 1,000 px of track.

**The Trunk, Motion A (industry, to planet and command) — AMEND.**
Co-signed with two amendments.
- The link range is 1,000 px, not 900 px. This matches Command's text, so there is one number across the coalition.
- Upkeep is per 1,000 px of track (1.5 IC/s), not per hub. A cheap hub should not be taxed twice. Clause (c), the +25% Extractor bonus on a node touching the Trunk, stays as the Planet contribution.

**Prepared Ground, Motion 3 (ground, to planet and naval) — AMEND.**
Co-signed only with this amendment: the terrain brush stays directly player-operated and free of any Sapper requirement. Sappers discount works to 60% IC and automate them when a battalion is present, but never gate them. Denying the existing 35 IC brush behind a unit would take away a toy the player already owns, which is an Article III problem. Seawall and Pump Station (PLANET-2) are buildable without Sappers. The `capLevels` exemption (L1505) applies to Sapper ditches too.

**The Navigable Moat, Motion B (naval, to planet) — AMEND.**
Co-signed with one amendment: the "Amphibious Lander from PLANET-X plus Trench Landers" line is one unit, the NAVAL-4 Trench Lander, as my Round 2 trade promised. There is no second lander in the roster. Everything else stands: CANAL brush at 90 IC per 105 px, no cap, `capLevels` exempt, a 10 s evacuation warning on Sluice Chains.

**The Deep Deposit, Motion C (naval, to industry and planet) — AMEND.**
Co-signed with one amendment: the survey node is the single `geosurvey` node from SCIENCE-5. Grade multiplies the INDUSTRY-2 tiers, it does not replace them. The seabed grade per water body uses `waterBounds` width as proposed. Yield is base × grade × (1 + 0.4 × tier), doubled at dig level 2 for land nodes.

**The Meridian Spine, Motion 3 (railgun, to planet and industry) — AMEND.**
Co-signed with one amendment: the Meridian barrel is built on the same Trunk graph, not on a second tube graph. It ships after the Trunk (the Trunk's `railnet` node is the prerequisite of `deeprail`). The 8 Mass-Driver segment threshold and the 60 s shot cycle stand.

## 4. Closing Statement

*The Minister rolls up the map, then unrolls it again, because three councils have drawn on it.*

Honourable members, a good parliament is judged by whether the map at the end matches the one at the start, and I intend to make sure it does not. We have a railway, a canal and a mine, all under one graph, one tide and one survey. The Admiralty has the water, the Engineers have the shovels, and Industry has the freight. I have the ground, and I lend it to all of you.

Build on it. Dig under it. Flood it. It will still be here.
