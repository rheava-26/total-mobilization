Levy boss trials: every round so far

Spectator on, levies on Free Rein, big platform arena with campfire and heart lantern. Times are median kill time for wins.

Read with caution.
  • These fights force the boss onto the levies. In your real game an unbeaten boss still targets you under Spectator (a bug being fixed now), so real fights are harder than this.
  • Brain "wins" in under 20s with ~0 damage taken were the Brain despawning (it flees outside the Crimson), not kills. They are marked ⚠ and are being re-run.
  • None of these levies have the new dodging and hook work yet.
  • Each row is only 1–18 runs. A fidelity audit of the test arena is in progress.
