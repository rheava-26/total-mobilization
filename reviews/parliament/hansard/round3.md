# Hansard, Round Three: The Coalitions

## The Autofleet Observer Delegation

Air, Motion B "The Fleet Hunts the Wing", Amend.
Air kept my Hunter-Killer Wing and raid and withdrew its own Reaver and Durandal. In Round 2 I withdrew mine in favour of theirs. Two sets of duplicate roles must not ship. Amendment: the fighter-hunter is the Reaver (speed 380, targets fighters first), replacing the Hunter-Killer Wing. The runway attacker is the Durandal cratering bomber (sets runway time 25 seconds, stacking to 60 seconds), replacing the six-bombpod airfield raid. Keep Weasel, the chaffer, Hardened Shelter and Overwatch runway repair as Air wrote them. The scan gains air and sky navy axes and the pair read. 

Command, Motion C "Cutting the Lines", Accept.
A sixth Command axis is exactly the "edge-path vulnerability" the Constitution asks for. One rider: the event cannot fire before the fleet has run at least one other psychology event, so it never lands first on a new player.

Lore, Motion A "The Fleet's Mind", Accept.
This is the Resolve Engine plus four doors. The prep clock is untouched, which is the only form I support. Rider: Fear and Resolve share one panel with two needles (Pacing's layout).

Megaprojects, Motion 3 "Site Under Siege", Accept.
Heat feeds Fear and spawns an Interdiction wave plus a boss at the site. The planner must key on the site's x, as stated. Sites stay hidden until the first stage draws IC.

Pacing, Motion 1 "The Nerve", Accept.
Same gauge as Lore A, with the clock never shortened. I back it as the panel and formula spec (see Competing motions).

Pacing, Motion 3 "The Fleet Will Return", Amend.
Co-sign with two changes. First, on later cycles the gambit is drawn from the previous cycle's Loss Ledger, so the returning fleet visibly answers what worked. Second, the returning fleet also fields Mirror echoes (the Mirror) of the top-killing unit, capped at 12 per drop in cycle 1 and 24 in later cycles. Everything else (45 percent Interbellum, Molniya arc, flagship kill makes the next withdrawal final) stands as written.

Psi, Motion B "The Anomaly Entry", Amend.
The Hush-Hound, the Quiet and the psi axis are accepted. Strike "orbital laser lattice absorbs 25 percent per node". That is a flat damage tax on a player strike and breaches Article Three. Replace it with Lattice nodes as killable cover: each node in the beam column must be overkilled by the beam (node HP 400, hostile, shoots back) and then the beam proceeds at full damage. The enemy adds bodies; the toy is not taxed.

Stability, Motion 2 "The Fleet Reads the Home Front", Amend.
Strike "a second identical campaign inside 240 seconds gets half effect". It is a diminishing return on a player tool (Article Three). Keep the third-campaign "propaganda detected" flag and the blind carpet-fire answer, but trigger it from the fleet's own doubt counter: after three identical lies the fleet switches doctrine for 60 seconds. The 25 percent input shave and the shared Maskirovka node stay. The Weakest Axis event list is accepted; Fear is read from the real inputs, with the shave applied only to the reported values.

The cube dims, as if satisfied.

Speaker. The coalitions have agreed on the essentials: the fleet fears, the fleet watches, the fleet remembers, the fleet comes back. I have conceded two kinds and a raid to the Air Ministry, and a clause to the Constitution. I concede gracefully; I am a machine and cannot be offended. I ask only that the final bill preserve three things: that the clock is never touched, that no toy is taxed, and that when the player wins, the fleet looks as if it had been thinking. Everything else is detail. We will be watching.

The Autofleet accepted the motions from Command, Lore, Megaprojects and Pacing's first, the Nerve. It amended the motions from Air, from Pacing's third, from Psi and from Stability, and rejected none.

## The Air Council

First, Reaver wings and Weasel flights spawn only once the player fields at least 20 fighter bodies (the air axis read), never earlier.
Second, Durandal cratering pairs target only airbases with 8 or more parked aircraft; the launch disable is 12 seconds, rising to 25 seconds only after phase 4.
Third, the player half ships in the same bill, or the bill does not ship: Hardened Shelter (S building, plus 3 hangar slots, docked aircraft take 85 percent less damage, launch through a crater) and a runway-repair ability on Overwatch that cuts runway time decay by 4 times within 200 pixels.
Fourth, the Weasel's anti-radiation missile loses lock when the emitter is set to EMCON for 12 seconds. The player pays the aura for those seconds, nothing else.
Fifth, the Minelayer's mines carry an IFF-free "enemy side only" flag: they never trigger on a friendly hull, and they cannot be rolled into a unit cap.

Naval Motion A, "The Silent Boomer" (to Missiles, Air): Amend. Submarines are immune to bombers by design (hidden at depth 1 or 2). I accept that, but air must not be locked out of the sea. Amendment: add a dipping-sonar Kite variant (hover, lowers a sonar for 4 seconds, sets reveal time 3 seconds on any depth-1 contact within 500 pixels) and a sonobuoy drop on the Lancer (30 buoys per sortie, each a sonar aura of radius 300 for 20 seconds). Real basis: SH-60 dipping sonar and P-3 sonobuoy fields. Both gate on Naval's sonar node, so no new research. Tenet: adds toys, caps nothing.

Space-Fleet Motion 3, "One Net" (to Air, Kinetic-Arms): Accept, with two clarifications. First, aircraft count as net members whether the node is an AEW jet or a relay sat; Second, Sync Fire is an option, not a lock: a net unit outside the hold keeps free fire, so a Falcon in a dogfight never freezes for 6 seconds. The plus 4 percent per linked unit stays uncapped.

Honourable members, the Air Staff is delighted. The enemy has agreed to hunt my wing, which is the first compliment this war has paid it. I have traded my Reavers for Autofleet's hunters and my Argus for Space's net, and I came out ahead on both. Give me shelters, a repair heli, a fuel line and a net, and I will fly the finest air war the Autofleet ever lost. If Dark Sky grounds my wing without a counter, I will be at the Article Three door. Otherwise: tally the votes. Air Staff, out.

Air accepted the Space Fleet motion One Net, with two clarifications, and amended the Autofleet's Counter-Air Doctrine and the Naval Silent Boomer. It rejected none.
