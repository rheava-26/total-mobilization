# AIR COUNCIL — Round 3 (Coalitions)

## 1. Answers

**Autofleet Motion A, "THE COUNTER-AIR DOCTRINE" (to Air, Naval): AMEND.** I co-sign with these exact amendments:
1. Reaver wings and Weasel flights spawn only once the player fields at least 20 fighter bodies (the `air` axis read), never earlier.
2. Durandal cratering pairs target only airbases with 8 or more parked aircraft; the launch disable is 12 s, rising to 25 s only after phase 4.
3. The player half ships in the same bill, or the bill does not ship: Hardened Shelter (S building, +3 hangar slots, docked aircraft take 85% less damage, launch through a crater) and a runway-repair ability on Overwatch (`helicmd`) that cuts `runwayT` decay by 4x within 200 px.
4. The Weasel's anti-radiation missile loses lock when the emitter is set to EMCON for 12 s. The player pays the aura for those seconds, nothing else.
5. The Minelayer's mines carry an IFF-free "enemy side only" flag: they never trigger on a friendly hull, and they cannot be rolled into a unit cap.

**Naval Motion A, "THE SILENT BOOMER" (to Missiles, Air): AMEND.** Submarines are immune to bombers by design (hidden at depth 1/2). I accept that, but air must not be locked out of the sea. Amendment: add a **dipping-sonar Kite variant** (hover, lowers a sonar for 4 s, sets `revealT` 3 s on any depth-1 contact within 500 px) and a **sonobuoy drop** on the Lancer (30 buoys per sortie, each a `sonar` aura of radius 300 for 20 s). Real basis: SH-60 dipping sonar and P-3 sonobuoy fields. Both gate on Naval's `sonar` node, so no new research. Size S. Tenet: adds toys, caps nothing.

**Space-Fleet Motion 3, "ONE NET" (to Air, Kinetic-Arms): ACCEPT, with two clarifications.** (a) Aircraft count as net members whether the node is an AEW jet or a relay sat; (b) SYNC FIRE is an option, not a lock: a net unit outside the hold keeps free fire, so a Falcon in a dogfight never freezes for 6 s. The +4% per linked unit stays uncapped.
