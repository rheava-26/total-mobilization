# Order Paper, Round 3: Coalitions

*The Clerk has carried every motion. All motions are in `reviews/parliament/motions_register.md`, grouped by the council that filed them. The Round 2 ballot is closed and tallied. The top of the ballot: ENERGY-1 Thermal Load 11 votes from 6 councils; AUTOFLEET-2 Fear Index 10; AUTOFLEET-3 Weakest Axis 8; LORE-1 Charter 8; INDUSTRY-1 Ammunition Trains 7.*

## Your task
Write `reviews/parliament/r3/<your-slug>.md` (under ~900 words) with these sections:
1. **Answers.** For EVERY motion in the register that names your council as an addressee, give a verdict:
   - **ACCEPT**: you co-sign as written;
   - **AMEND**: you co-sign only with the exact amendment you state;
   - **REJECT**: one-line reason.

   Search the register for your slug and for your council's name. Motions may spell it either way.
2. **Joins.** Motions not addressed to you that you want to co-sign anyway, with one line each.
3. **Competing motions.** Several councils filed near-identical motions, for example four rail-network motions and three forecast motions. Name the version you back and say why, so the Speaker can merge them into one coalition bill.
4. **Closing statement.** In character, ~100 words.

Edit no other file. When done, reply with your verdicts as a compact list in the form `motion title (filer) — ACCEPT/AMEND/REJECT`.
