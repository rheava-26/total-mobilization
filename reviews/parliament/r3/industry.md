## 2. Joins

- **Motion 1 "The One Locker" (kinetic-arms, to railgun and energy).** Non-default rounds draw `munitions` at 0.04 per shot. This is my stock system with a second customer, and a shortage costs IC, not stats.
- **Motion 2 "The Grid" (energy, to railgun and space-fleet).** The power pool with IC debt at 0.6 IC/s per MW is the same logistics idea for electricity: fire is never throttled, the bill is the cost.

## 3. Competing motions

- **Rail net (Command B, my Motion A, Planet P-2, Railgun Meridian Spine).** I back **P-2**. It is the cleanest text: link range 1,200 px, five named layers, and it already reserves the tube segments for the Meridian. Command's Molodets Train rides on it as an add-on, and the Meridian is a later doctrine on the same graph.
- **Ledger engine (Command A, Market A, my Motion B, New-player 3).** I back **Command A** as the skeleton: one loop, three skins. Market A's reserve formula and kills-per-IC spender become the market skin. New-player 3's status chips are mandatory on all faces.
- **Wreck economy (Science Motion 1, Lore B, my Motion C).** I back **Science Motion 1**. It has the 700 px Salvage Yard radius, the family bonus at 4 dossiers, and the Xenotech reveal in one text.
- **Deposits (Naval C, Planet P-3, INDUSTRY-2, Bio B).** Merge into one coalition bill: P-3 is the yield formula, Naval C adds the seabed buildings, INDUSTRY-2 gives the tiers, Bio B is the Uranium customer.

## 4. Closing statement

Mr Speaker, the Ministry asked for one thing: somewhere for the money to go that the enemy can hit. This House has delivered a net of rails, a ledger, a salvage yard and a mine in the sea. Every one of them is a target and a reason to defend it. I have signed what I can and amended what I must; I ask only that the Clerk build the rail graph once and the ledger once. Three of anything is a bureaucracy, and I am a quartermaster, not a bureaucrat. Let the freight run.
