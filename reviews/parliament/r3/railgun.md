## 2. Joins

- **Motion P-2 — THE CONTINENTAL RAIL GRAPH (planet; to industry, command).** I co-sign. Its clause (e) already carries the tube as Meridian barrel. My own "THE MERIDIAN SPINE" motion is withdrawn into it.
- **THE TRUNK (industry, Motion A).** Co-sign on its Orbital Resupply combo, with one rider. A **Launch Rail** (RAILGUN-4) counts as a rail-linked Space Center for free lift, so a Space Gun reseeds the grid with no rocket part.
- **Energy "THE THERMAL FIELD" / physics "THE THERMAL LEDGER" / psi "THE HEAT BOOK".** I join the heat coalition in principle. Rails are `killBy` kinetic (L6680), so heated targets take the bonus automatically, and rails take no heat themselves.
- **Missiles' fire-direction motion (to space-fleet, kinetic-arms).** Co-sign. A Long Gun shot synchronised with a satellite salvo is the Time-on-Target the gun was born for.

## 3. Competing Motions

- **Rail networks (P-2 planet, THE TRUNK industry, "The Trunk" command, my Meridian Spine).** I back **P-2**.
  - It is the only version that names the barrel use.
  - It carries (a) freight stock, (b) trunk bonus, (c) upkeep, (d) buried-tube immunity and (e) the Meridian tube in one graph pass.
  - It keeps one overlay and one set of weak points. The Speaker can lift the Molodets train from command's version as an add-on unit.
  - Industry's version supplies the research node (`railnet`, tier 1, cost 100) and I support that node as the one gate.
- **Superheavy gun (megaprojects Motion 1, kinetic-arms Motion 3, game-feel Motion A, my RAILGUN-2/X).** I back **megaprojects Motion 1 as amended above** as the chassis. Kinetic-arms and game-feel motions become riders on it, and the Speaker should merge all four into one bill.
- **Capacitor / power (energy THE GRID, my RAILGUN-1).** I back **THE GRID as amended**. One bank, one pool, one Foundry.
- **Ammunition (kinetic-arms ONE LOCKER, my RAILGUN-3, KINETIC-2).** I back **ONE LOCKER**. It is the complete form, and I have nothing left to add.

## 4. Closing Statement

Honourable members. We came as three guns and leave as one barrel, one bank and one locker of rounds, with a wire laid under the whole continent for good measure. Nobody in this House shot anybody. The Directorate is moved, and a little suspicious. Take the Long Gun. Build it in sections. Pay its bill in IC and love it in public. When the Autofleet's flagship hangs over the capital and you hear that low rolling crack arrive a full second after the light, remember that it was a coalition that fired it. Seconded. Loudly.
