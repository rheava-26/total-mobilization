# MARKET COUNCIL — Round 3 Coalition Paper

## 1. Answers

**Command, Motion A "The Standing Plan" (to industry, market): AMEND.**
Co-sign with two amendments. (a) The Exchange dividend is **+10% build value on market-spent IC**, not "-2%". A negative dividend makes the market road strictly worse than a plain Autofoundry, and no one would build it. The overhead (+6%) stays with the Bureau only. (b) The Exchange spends only IC above `R`, and `R = 800*(1-max(mkt,cmd))+150` is the *default* for every ledger; the player may override `R` per ledger by hand. This is the engine I back (see section 3).

**Industry, Motion B "The Five-Year Ledger" (to command, market): AMEND.**
Co-sign if the Exchange stays a **separate building** that raises `G.mkt` and spends surplus on its own. Putting MARKET only as a *line mode* on a Planning Bureau means a market society needs a Bureau, which is the Command road wearing a hat. Amendment: MARKET mode may be selected on any ledger line, but an Exchange running with no ledger at all must still outflow surplus. The "5% reserve penalty" on MARKET lines is dropped; the dividend is already the trade.

**New-Player, Motion 1 "One Dial, Five Faces" (to market, pacing): ACCEPT.**
`G.forecast` with `sigma/sqrt(1+n_obs)` is the object I wanted. I withdraw MARKET-2's own display and keep only the positions panel as its fourth renderer.

**Pacing, Motion 2 "The One Forecast" (to market, new-player): ACCEPT.**
Same object. The prep beats at 70/45/25/8/4% are good theatre. Positions ship second, as stated.

**Psi, Motion C "The Listening" (to market, science): AMEND.**
Co-sign with: (a) the Listening Chamber counts as an extra observer **only for its 90 s**, then lapses; sharpening that never expires would remove the cost of precision. (b) Positions staked during a listen settle at the *post-sharpening* displayed `p`, so a peeking player cannot farm free payout. (c) The "1 point per 2 positions held" discount stays. `listenDebt` stays psi's.

**Science, Motion 3 "The Unified Forecast" (to market, lore): AMEND.**
Co-sign with: low-confidence wire lines are wrong with probability `0.25*(1-conf)`, not a flat 25%, so a well-instrumented player is not still fed a quarter lies. Intent labels never lie (PACING-2 rule). Research key `futures` stays; `signals` (def) is the shared sensor node.

## 4. Closing Statement

Mr Speaker, the House did what markets do: it discovered, without being told, that five councils wanted the same forecast and four wanted the same ledger. Prices converge. I ask the Speaker only to remember that a treasury which sits is a treasury that rots, and that every idle IC in report 7 was a vote the player never cast. Pass the Standing Plan. Pass the one forecast. Let the sky keep its debris, and let the surplus find its own way home.
