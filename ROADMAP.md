## ⭐ THE DESIGN TENET (read this first, it governs every decision)

> **The game says YES to the player's power fantasy. Difficulty comes from the enemy being overwhelming — NEVER from capping, nerfing, or denying the player their toys.**

The designer's own words: *"you see this thing and say 'oooh I could place 100 of those and it'd be OP?' and then the game says 'errr no.' We say yes."* When something is too strong, the answer is a scarier enemy or a real cost (upkeep/logistics), **not** a hard cap or diminishing returns. Multiplicative aura stacking, 90 airships, a planet-covering army, a wall-of-light particle cannon — all intentional. Let them happen. Fix *perf* and *bugs*, never the fantasy.

## ⛔→⚖ NO GAMEPLAY CAPS ON MASS (2026-08-20 ruling) — DELIBERATELY REVERSED to a WEIGHTED CAP (v3.98.0, 2026-08-28)

**HISTORICAL RULING (2026-08-20, superseded below — kept for the record):** Designer reconsidered and REJECTED combat-width / re-capping Shapeshifters+Clones: *"one of the best parts is having thousands of units... powerful computers can easily handle it."* Combat width & army-cap-on-off-cap-units were ruled GAMEPLAY CAPS → REJECTED. Thousands of units IS the game; any limit on how many FIGHT changes what the game is = tenet #1 violation. Perf was ruled device-dependent and an engine-only problem (LOD/battlegroups), never a gameplay change.

**★ v3.98.0 — THE RULING WAS DELIBERATELY REVERSED, NOT VIOLATED BY ACCIDENT.** What actually happened in play wasn't "a big satisfying army" — it was **thousands of essentially-free drones** (cdrone/kdrone/android/clone/shapeshifter-family units carried NO army-cap cost at all, see `isMechanical`'s old cap-exemption) spammed to ~9,000, dropping the game to ~3fps and washing every mass-death fight into a solid white explosion sheet. That isn't the power fantasy delivering scale — it's a legibility/perf EXPLOIT wearing the power fantasy's clothes: you can't see or feel 9,000 individually-worthless units the same way you feel a real combined-arms army. Per tenet #1 the fix is still never "cap/nerf/deny the toys" outright — so instead of a headcount ban, **v3.98.0 ships a WEIGHTED army cap**: EVERY unit counts against the cap now (drones, mechs, clones, ground/naval/sky capitals, superweapons — nothing is exempt any more, closing the loophole `v3.91.1`'s Shapeshifters-only fix started), but they count DIFFERENTLY — a drone squadron costs 0.35 weight, a normal line unit costs 1, a capital ship costs 4-6, a superweapon costs 5-7 (`deriveWeight()`/`d.wt`, ~L3313). The BUDGET (`armyCap()`) is raised well past its old headcount value and stays MP+Housing-driven — mass is still fully earned, just priced instead of free. Headless-tuned so a real few-hundred-unit combined-arms army (incl. capitals) comfortably affords ~half the budget, while pure drone-spam still walls out at **~1,850 drones** (was unlimited/~9,000) — perf-safe, but still unmistakably a swarm, not a headcount ban on any one unit type. Explosion glow also got a hard per-frame brightness clamp (`GLOW_SOFT_CAP`) so a mass-death event literally cannot wash the screen to white any more, independent of the cap. **Full writeup + tuned numbers: see the "WEIGHTED ARMY CAP" entry below.** The 2026-08-20 engine-only tools (LOD, battlegroups) remain valid and un-superseded as the answer to remaining perf cost — this reversal is orthogonal, about legibility/exploit-closing at the top end, not a retreat from "yes to scale."

## ⚙ BATTLEGROUPS — THE ANSWER TO MASS (designer direction, 2026-08-20) ★ eventual, deferred

**Designer's vision (verbatim gist):** *"i wanna do battlegroups eventually where squads upon squads will form unit battalions of the same type."* So the hierarchy becomes **body → squad → BATTALION (a battlegroup of same-type squads)** — a battalion is ONE entity with a count/HP pool + formation, simulated & rendered as a cluster that READS as many. This is the tenet-correct way to say YES to thousands: the player fields battalions, the engine simulates dozens of entities, not thousands of independent bodies. (Aligns with the HOI4-style battlegroup idea already banked in the feedback tracker — now the confirmed direction.)

**WHY (perf diagnosis, profiled 2026-08-20 from report_5 — a 3,791-unit game running at ~0.29x speed / "every second like 3.5s"):**

## 🧠 THE TRANSCENDENCE ARC — combos become DOCTRINES, not units (designer design pass, 2026-08-17)

**This is the fix for v1's single biggest missed opportunity: "a combo only ever unlocked a unit — it never changed the world."** The designer wants every research tree "infinitely more impactful," converging on a post-human capstone. Banked in full so nothing evaporates. NOT yet approved for build — framework-first, content-incremental (see feasibility note).

**🔻 DESIGNER PIVOT (2026-08-17, same session):** designer pulled BACK from going all-in on the tree-deepening — rightly worried it's a whole second meta-layer on a game whose #1 complaint was "too complex to learn" (cost us the entire v3.35 tutorial to claw back). **DECISION: do NOT deepen all the research trees.** Instead — (a) make the BASE game feel stronger first, and (b) deliver civilization-scale change through **MEGASTRUCTURES revealed/activated via EVENT CHAINS**, not through tree nodes. This is a BETTER container for the best part of the arc: the compute-economy-whose-cost-is-fragility becomes LITERAL (the megastructure on the map IS the building the enemy decapitates), and it's one legible object + a story beat instead of a lattice of combos. Reuses the dormant Super Events engine (`EVENTS_LIVE=false`) — that engine was waiting for exactly this content. Caveat banked: megastructures RELOCATE the cost (mechanic cheap, event-chain WRITING is the same big lift) into the STORY domain the designer wants to own — good division of labor (agents build mechanics + wire events; designer writes beats at their own pace). **STATUS: Transcendence arc PARKED as "megastructure + event chain, someday." Near-term = base-strength work (see below).**

**➡ CHOSEN NEAR-TERM DIRECTION (designer picked, 2026-08-17): "the ENEMY feels threatening" — the autofleet threat pass.** Per the tenet this is the ONLY real difficulty lever. Standing problem: enemy trivially easy (800 fighters delete the invasion; "10% of tax income repels it"). **KEY REFRAME (designer's own prior steer + perf reality): scary = SMART + AGGRESSIVE, NOT numerous. The "scale fleet to 1000+" framing is DEAD** (phone can't sim thousands; designer wants ~equal numbers + smart enemies). Contained first slice (NOT v4.0 orbital-siege megasystem): (1) **ADAPTIVITY — highest leverage** — enemy reads your composition and drops the COUNTER (air→AA-fairing flak; turtle→bunker-busters + long-range city-sniping artillery; navy→orbital rods) → kills the spam-one-toy dominant strategy, forces response. (2) **First strike that bites + telegraphed** (even Probe feels apocalyptic in its window) AND fixes the fairness flip-side (opening volley currently UN-defendable → give it a telegraph + interceptable window so good play answers it — playtest item #2). (3) **Aggression not sponginess** — focus-fire + high-value targeting (airbases/super-units), bosses get destructible subsystems not fatter HP. SEQUENCING: land the two in-flight lanes (capital fortress + squad) FIRST, open the enemy lane on a clean file. **FRAMING (designer, 2026-08-17): "less a rework and more CONTINUING to work on it" — layer meaner behavior onto the EXISTING enemy systems (escalation v2, adaptive buster-director, opening bombarders) incrementally and TUNE against playtest; do NOT demolish + rebuild. Small increments, telegraphed + tunable, iterate. Iteration is the safety rail against "adaptive = feels like cheating."**

**The capstone — TRANSCENDENCE (the structurally strongest idea):** a late, expensive, ONE-WAY opt-in that DISSOLVES manpower and re-pegs your army cap to a COMPUTATION economy. It does NOT raise the manpower number — it rips the governor out. Army cap becomes `f(compute)` sourced from infrastructure (Comms Towers, Data Centers, quantum cores), with **no upper limit** → spam hundreds of units. THEME-PERFECT: you mobilized so hard you stopped being made of people; your only limit is silicon you can defend.
- **⚠ MANPOWER CONSTRAINT (do NOT violate):** designer said manpower is "perfect where it is right now" / "definitely don't want to change the way manpower works." Reconciliation Claude proposed and designer implicitly building on: base manpower stays 100% untouched for everyone; Transcendence is an opt-in doctrine to ESCAPE manpower, not a change to it. CONFIRM this reading before building — if designer means manpower literally persists under it, redesign.
- **The cost is FRAGILITY, not a cap (tenet-compliant).** No ceiling, but the "cap" LIVES IN BUILDINGS. Bomb the compute network (data centers/comms/cores) → ceiling collapses → units go dormant/offline/berserk. The more godlike you get, the more your whole power sits in a few high-value nodes the autofleet can decapitate. *"You're a god, but your brain is in a building, and they know where it is."* Spatial, attackable, dramatic — strictly better drama than the manpower number. Reuses upkeep mechanic (compute-upkeep = reskin).

**The FORK (the society/policy shift the game keeps almost having) — a real identity choice, both reach Transcendence flavored differently:**
- **ASCENSION (bio/psi — keep the humans):** your PEOPLE uploaded/linked. Units = extensions of living minds: adaptive, high-skill, but tied to stability/population; EW/EMP makes them PANIC (there's a mind in there). Society → transcendent verdant.
- **SINGULARITY (automation/AI — build the minds):** you MANUFACTURE intelligence. Fearless, endlessly copyable, but spoofable/hackable, no improvisation. Thematically loaded — the autofleet IS a rogue machine intelligence → *"are you becoming the thing you fight?"* Society → machine.

**The ladder up to it (designer's sketch, cleaned):**
- **Psi × Robotics = Neural Integration** → brain-chips / mechanical frames; remote-piloted frames (no pilot to lose) = first node of the mind-network.
- **Neural Integration × Bio = Genetic / Neural Engineering** → selective gene editing, swap bodies on the fly mid-battle, CONSUMPTIONLESS bodies (energy-only, drop food/water logistics → economic shift).
- **Applied Psionics × Neural Integration = Mechanical Integration / Remote Consciousness** → link one mind into a jet remotely; one consciousness, many bodies. **NOTE: this is mechanically the SQUAD SYSTEM (one brain, many hitboxes) — the squad code is literally the seed of this fantasy; at the high end the squad becomes a doctrine (your whole army = one mind wearing a hundred shells).**
- Human-conscious-integration vs AI-systems = the fork above; "entirely interconnected battlefield/technology" is the payoff both share.

**Two design constraints (keep the whole arc canon-clean & shippable):**
1. **Bio & psi are "just as powerful" — but pointed INWARD.** Canon (designer's own correction): nothing biological hurts a pure-machine autofleet. So bio's power = rewrite YOUR OWN forces (regen, dirt-cheap super-soldiers, swappable/consumptionless bodies), never "infect the enemy." Psi likewise (buffs, own-network control, remote-piloting). Every bit as strong — strong at transforming YOU, not damaging THEM.
2. **The mechanics are CHEAP; the CONTENT is the "SO much work."** Engine hooks already exist: `society()` repaints world + adds modifier (fork reuses it); army cap is a formula (swap `mp` term → `compute` term); upkeep exists (compute-upkeep reskin); combos exist as prereq lists (just need to fire a TRANSFORMATION not only unlock a unit); `mult`/`gmod` global-modifier system + building `gmod` can feed a `G.compute` pool. **RECOMMENDATION (Claude, strong): build the FRAMEWORK once — combo-fires-a-doctrine + compute economy + society fork + cap-swap capstone (~a weekend) — then pour content in incrementally. Do NOT deepen all 7 branches at once; make the psi→robotics→bio spine genuinely transformative as the PROOF, and if it lands it's the template for the rest.** That's how this ships instead of stalling.

## 😰 MORALE / ORGANIZATION — "shock" on the battlefield (designer concept, needs a design pass)
Make stability VISCERAL on the ground. A unit-level **morale/organization** state that mirrors the STABILITY score: when the nation is stable, units fight crisp and organized; when stability craters — because a city block just got leveled and a chunk of the defensive network crumbled — units on the ground **panic and enter SHOCK**, struggling to focus. Designer's framing: *"I'd be freaking the hell out and struggling to focus if I just watched all of Prague get erased off the map."*
- **Driven by stability + shock events:** baseline morale follows `effStab`; a catastrophe (city DISTRICT destroyed, a big defensive structure lost, a nuke nearby) fires a temporary SHOCK SPIKE — a jolt of panic that fades over seconds. Possibly localized (units near the catastrophe shaken hardest) + a global baseline.
- **What shock does:** shaken units fire slower, aim worse (lead/accuracy degrade — they were just given leading; panic undoes it), move hesitantly/scatter, maybe visually tremble. Recovers as stability climbs back and no new shocks land.
- **This is the GRADIENT the designer always wanted:** stability loss should "degrade your units," not instant-loss. The Capitulation Crisis (production freeze + ~30% mutiny at 0 stab) is the rock-bottom extreme; morale is the smooth ramp above it.
- **NO death spiral (critical):** because stability is now cap-based and RECOVERABLE (not a drain), morale recovers with it — a shaken army claws back, it doesn't spiral to zero with no counterplay (the exact thing the designer hated about the old drain). Keep a floor so low morale never = helpless.
- **IMPACT VARIES HEAVILY by unit + situation** (v2 detail):
  - **MECHANICAL units are immune** (drones, androids, mechs, autonomous, machine-family). **Organic/human units falter.** This is the strategic core: a robot army ignores morale entirely (the "machine" society answer).
  - **Faltering behaviors (a spectrum):** hesitate / can't pick a target for a moment · hunker down & get SUPPRESSED · full mental breakdown / lose composure · and at minimum become WASTEFUL — dump their whole magazine in a panic, accidentally over-fire missiles (burns ammo/upkeep, reads as desperation, not just "weaker").
  - **⭐ EVERY RESEARCH PATH is secretly a morale answer** (the insight that makes the tree cohere): Automation → immune robots · Superhumans → fearless demigods · Psionics → mental fortitude/conditioning · Biotech → engineered composure · Propaganda/defensive structures → SUPPRESS panic in a radius. This finally makes the research tree about the game's real question: *how does your civilization keep functioning while watching itself be annihilated?* — the "what does total mobilization turn you into" theme, made mechanical. (Answers the design doc's "societal shift was never represented.")
  - **Shield-watching stress (a PSYCHOLOGICAL input, NOT a shield nerf):** shields stay 100% mechanically identical — same HP/blocking/protection, zero functional downside. The ONLY effect is a MORALE stressor on ORGANIC units sheltering under a shield that's visibly taking hits/wavering (they're objectively safer but rattled watching it weaken). Flows through the normal immunity rules — mechanical units / supermen under a shield don't flinch. You'd still build every shield you can; this is pure dread-flavor on the morale ledger, never a reason to avoid shields.
  - **PRODUCTION shock:** factory/economy workers panic under catastrophe too (9/11 framing — "let me check if my family's okay"), slowing production. The gradient above the Capitulation Crisis's hard production-freeze.
  - **Ties into the DECISION/SOCIETY layer:** dystopian / "mean" doctrine choices interact with morale (a harsher society may suppress panic at a human cost, etc.).
- **UNIFIED PLAN — actual roster susceptibility** (from real `UNIT_DEFS`/`UNIT_FAM`):
  - **IMMUNE (no mind to break):** ALL buildings + emplacement/city guns · drones (`cdrone`, `kdrone`) · robots (`android`, `colossus`) · electronic/orbital platforms (`railgun`, `satlaser`, `missilesat`, `rods`, `spacefrigate`, `ostrikefighter`, `dreadnought`) · nano-constructs (`airship` "Nanofortress", `shifter` nanoswarm).
  - **RESISTANT (engineered/conditioned):** `superhuman` (fearless demigods) · `psitrooper` (psionic fortitude) · `cyborg` (part-machine) · `titan` (part-machine leviathan) · `shock` (gene-conditioned). `clone` = DESIGN CHOICE (eerily-calm bred-numb = resistant + creepy, OR expendable fodder = susceptible — Fable leans resistant/creepy).
  - **SUSCEPTIBLE (ordinary humans — FEEL every catastrophe):** conventional ground (`inf`, `scout`, `tank`, `heavytank`, `aa`, `arty`, `mlrs`, `sam`) · ALL naval crews (`destroyer`→`carrier`) · ALL air crews (`fighter`, `strikejet`, `pdjet`, `bomber`).
  - **⭐ THIS MAPS ONTO THE TREE:** start conventional = a fully susceptible army that feels the war → Automation = immune machine army → Superhumans/Psionics = fearless army → Biotech = conditioned. Your tech path literally decides how much your people feel the horror. That IS the "what does total mobilization turn you into" theme.
- **Fable's design contributions:** (a) high morale could give a small BONUS (crisp/disciplined = +accuracy), so stability is a positive lever not just downside-avoidance; (b) **Propaganda Bureau + Comms Tower should project a morale-STEADYING aura** (suppress panic in radius) — finally gives them a battlefield role beyond the stability cap; (c) dystopia/"mean" doctrine = suppress panic by FEAR (units don't break, but you pay in humanity/stability-cap).
- **BUILD IN LAYERS** (like garrison/stability were): L1 spine = per-organic-unit morale from `effStab` + shock spikes on catastrophe → combat faltering (mechanical immune) + clear feedback (shaken visual + HUD). L2 = research suppression counters. L3 = production shock + shield-stress + society ties.
- **Legibility is make-or-break:** the player MUST see why units falter — shaken-unit visual (tremble/hunker/scatter), a morale HUD readout, a "⚠ SHOCK — [district] LOST" jolt notification.

### 🧩 SQUADS — collision rework (designer's revised model; re-touches recent squad work)
Current model = one entity + footprint collision (reads as a blob). Designer wants: **separate collision + separate hitboxes per body, but a shared "brain" (targeting/AI) + a cohesion "pull force"** keeping the bodies clustered. Tightly linked, mostly independent. NOTE: separate collision reintroduces per-body cost — mitigate by keeping the AI/targeting shared (the expensive part stays single) + the existing render LOD.

### 🧵 MULTICORE / WORKERS — Fable's honest verdict: probably NOT worth it
Designer asked to offload targeting math to a worker so the main thread just renders. Reality: Workers can't touch the live object graph — you'd `postMessage`-copy all entity positions out and results back every frame; for thousands of entities that serialization usually costs ~what it saves and adds a frame of targeting latency. Profiling says **RENDERING is the bottleneck, not targeting math** — so the high-value fix is render-side (invisible bullets/tracers, LOD, glow cheapening), not multicore. Revisit only if targeting math ever becomes the profiled hot spot.

### 🎮 "PLAY AS THE AUTOFLEET" + TWO-PLANE MULTIPLAYER (designer idea — great fantasy, honest costs)
Designer: "one player playing the autofleet would be super fun!" — and it's right (asymmetric besieger-vs-besieged is proven fun; the game already has a rich decision-making attacker AI to inherit). **Fable's honest cost read (why 2P is a 2.0/sequel-scale bet, NOT near-term):** (1) **NETCODE is the monster** — this is a single-file, client-side, zero-network game; real-time 1v1 needs state sync / relay-or-P2P / latency handling = arguably a bigger lift than the whole rest of the roadmap combined. (2) **Attacker has nothing to do during PREP** → you'd have to design+balance a whole SECOND (attacker) economy/build-up game. (3) **Asymmetric balance is a forever-job** vs a human who exploits/focus-fires/times pushes; SP tuning won't transfer.
- **★★ THE ACTUAL BUILD PATH (designer's refinement — solves the defender-AI problem entirely): SIEGE A SAVED DEFENSE.** No 2P, no AI. The defender is a **SAVED SNAPSHOT of a real base** — and a base already runs its OWN guns (emplacements/CIWS/garrison/interceptors/shields all auto-fire), so no defender AI is needed at all: it's a self-defending fortress.   - **SNEAKY UNLOCK — async social w/o netcode:** because a defense is just a SAVED FILE, you can attack SOMEONE ELSE'S saved base (share fortress → friend assaults → compare scores). ~80% of the 2P social juice, zero networking.
  - **THE ONE DEPENDENCY: a base SAVE/SERIALIZE system** (game is currently session-only). Snapshot `G.buildings`/units/terrain/garrison → JSON → restore into a fresh battle. Bounded work, and it gives normal save/load as a bonus. Attacker toolkit = reuse the existing STRAT/strike system. THIS is the path (supersedes the earlier "SP vs defender-AI" idea).
