Levy: Thorium — class compatibility for the Levy militia.

Soldiers play Thorium Bard instruments and use Thorium Healer weapons as intended.
Requires the Levy mod and Thorium.

Bards: a soldier holding an instrument fires its notes at enemies and spends Inspiration
from a personal pool that refills on Thorium's own regeneration wheel. The pool's size
follows the commander's Inspiration Crystals. An empty pool means the soldier holds until
it refills.

Healers: a soldier holding an ally-healing weapon heals wounded soldiers of the same
commander within reach, using the weapon's own heal amount, and never wastes a heal on
an enemy.
