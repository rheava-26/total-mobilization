# Round 3 Paper: Ministry of Megaprojects (`megaprojects`)

## 1. Answers

**THE BROOD PIT (filed by bio, Motion C) — AMEND.**
The Pit has "4 sections". That is a cap on a megaproject. Amendment: the Pit is an uncapped chain. Each section adds one larva seed slot and one Cooling Spine tick, and the first four sections are the named stages (Vat Hall, Biomass Silo, Cooling Spine, Seed Chamber). Sections beyond four reuse Seed Chamber. Interdiction heat is computed per section, as in my Site Under Siege motion, so a bigger Pit draws a bigger fleet. With that, we co-sign.

**ONE LONG GUN (filed by kinetic-arms, Motion 3) — ACCEPT.**
KINETIC-X is withdrawn and folds into the Long Gun. We take the Annihilator-charge interrupt and the sabot slot. HARP and SHARP become section flavour text.

**THE LONG GUN COALITION (filed by railgun, Motion 1) — ACCEPT.**
We back this as the base text. It strikes the Recoil Cradle penalty (good) and keeps both scalings linear and uncapped. One amendment: one node, `megastructures`, no `supergun` sub-unlock, to keep the research tree to a single new entry.

**THE LONG SHOT (filed by game-feel, Motion A) — AMEND.**
Add: the Warhead Cam, Hush and map-wide flash fire only on the first shot in any 20 s window, so a rapid battery of guns never freezes the game. Shot rate and damage untouched. Skippable and off under `reducedMotion`, as filed. With that, we co-sign.

**ORION: THE SHIP AND THE PROJECT (filed by space-fleet, Motion 2) — ACCEPT.**
The three stages and the hull are as filed. One note for the Clerk: uranium per pulse is a `{type:'uranium'}` requirement in `projReqProgress`, a new req type that my framework bill (MEGA-1) must carry.

## 2. Joins

- **THE MERIDIAN SPINE (filed by railgun, to planet/industry).** The tube barrel extends the Long Gun, so I co-sign.
- **THE CONTINENTAL RAIL GRAPH (filed by industry/command, P-2).** It is the same graph, and I want one pass.
- **THE ONE FORECAST / THE UNIFIED FORECAST.** I have no stake, but a megaproject's heat should be a visible reading on the forecast. I co-sign whichever version carries a "site heat" line.

## 3. Competing Motions

- **Superheavy gun.** I back railgun's THE LONG GUN COALITION as the single text, with game-feel's Long Shot as a rider and kinetic-arms' ONE LONG GUN merged in. Reason: it is the most complete (ammo tiers, slew, upkeep, N >= 40 gate) and it already withdraws the cap-like cradle penalty. My own filed motion (THE LONG GUN, ONE GUN) is withdrawn in its favour.
- **Rail networks.** I back P-2 (THE CONTINENTAL RAIL GRAPH) as the one graph, and the Meridian Spine as its later Long Gun layer. Reason: one graph pass and one set of weak points is cheaper than four.
- **Orion.** One text only: space-fleet's Motion 2. My own Motion 2 is withdrawn in its favour, since the two are the same bill.
- **Site Under Siege.** My Motion 3 stands, addressed to autofleet and lore. I ask the Speaker to merge it with AUTOFLEET-2 so heat is one term of the Fear Index.

## 4. Closing Statement

Madam Speaker, I came to this House with five bills and I leave it with one gun, one ship, one graph and a siege. That is what a coalition is: a wonder built by many hands. The fleet is afraid of an industrial Earth, and tonight this House has voted to be afraid-making. Lift the framework. Build the first section. Put a wall of guns around it, because they are coming for it, and let them come.
