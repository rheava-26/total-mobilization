# Travel lab (A to B)

Measures how well levies get from one point to another: a map-point order (Barracks "Send to map point", Command Radio shift-click) or Follow behind a commander who walks the same route.
It is a harness scenario (`LEVY_HARNESS_SCENARIO=travel`, `Common/Testing/CombatHarness.Travel.cs`) plus small scripts here. It is separate from the boss lab (`levylab`) because it needs long courses, not arenas.

## No-code travel test (`travelab`, round 8)
A travel test is one small yaml file and one command; no C#, no run scripts. A levy that teleports (the catch-up hop, or any jump of 6+ tiles in one tick) FAILS: the hop is off by default (`hop: off`, nobody watches the army) and every jump is counted anyway.
```
export TRAVELAB_HOME=/home/user/lab/travelab                      # scratch: a tML copy, generated worlds, builds, runs (first use copies /home/user/tml-bin/tml)
levy-mod/lab/travel/travelab run levy-mod/lab/travel/specs/hellevator.yaml [--tmod X.tmod] [-j 3] [--keep]   # builds levy-mod/Levy once per content hash, generates the world once, one boot per cell, one table
```
The table has one row per arm x goal x gear tier: arrive (arrived and never teleported) / teleported / trip seconds / stuck episodes (levies) / max depth, then one line per run. Spec keys: `world` ({seed, size} or {file} or a cached name), `start` (spawn | X | [X, Y]),
`goal` or `goals` (far | east:N | west:N | surface:X | underworld | cave[:depth] | [X, Y]), `edit` (`hellevator: {dx, width, platforms, ring}`), `levies`, `gear` (counts per tier: bare boots wings hook wh climb prehm hm endgame, or `kit:HermesBoots+CloudinaBottle+hk=GrapplingHook`), `runs`, `spread`, `timeout` (game seconds), `hop`, `env`,
`arms` (A/B: each arm overrides any key, plus `tmod:` and `env: {LEVY_TRAVEL8: 0}`). A new test is a new yaml (`specs/hellevator.yaml` is the example). Under it: `LEVY_TRAVEL_START / GOAL / EDIT / RUNS / SPREAD` and `kit:` gear in `CombatHarness.TravelSpec.cs`, the same `CombatHarness.Travel.cs` scenario, `levies.csv` / `events.txt` per run in `runs/<spec>_<tag>/<arm>__<goal>/out/rN/`.
`LEVY_TRAVEL8=0` turns the round 8 movement changes off in the same build. `env: {LEVY_TRAVEL_DBG: "from,to,step,levy"}` prints a DBG line per sampled tick in events.txt (position, velocity, route state, `dropWhy`, `flyWhy`, waypoint kinds).

### Hold the banners (`order: banner`, `edit: tower`, `hold: N`)
`order: banner` places a real Holding Banner (WorldGen.PlaceObject, the tile entity as the placement hook does) on the floor of the goal at tick 120 and gives the army the Hold the banners order; a levy ARRIVES when it stands at its own stand inside the banner's aura (and never teleported).
`edit: [{tower: {dx, height, width, stairs, top}}]` builds a free-standing tower dx tiles east of the start (a zigzag of platforms every `stairs` rows, `stairs: 0` = a sheer stone pillar; `top: platform | brick`) and `goal: tower` is the middle of its top. `hold: N` keeps the run going after everyone has arrived: for N seconds zombies, slimes and demon eyes come at the banner from 15 tiles either side (levies armed) and events.txt gets a `HOLDREPORT` (percent of the time in the aura and on the stand, the farthest stray, chases past 8 tiles, hits, stands at the end) and a `BANNERREPORT` (buffed, distinct stands).
Specs: `specs/banner_towers.yaml` (ground control, a platform floor 14 up, a 35-row tower with platform stairs, a 35-row pillar), `specs/banner_hold.yaml`.

## What it records (per levy, `out/levies.csv`)
arrived / arrival time, straight line vs tiles actually walked (`ratio`), time stuck (no 4-tile exit from a spot for 3 s, only while still travelling), stall (best progress toward the goal not improved for 10 s), where (course feature / world x),
teleports (a jump of more than 6 tiles in one tick), falls of 8+ tiles, seconds wet, body lost, and the order/state it ended in. `events.txt` has every STUCK / TELEPORT / FALL / "order ended" with the movement reason, an ASCII map around the first stuck levy of each gear, the route-planner log and cost counters.
`trace.csv` is a position every half second (x, elevation, state); `surface.csv` the standing surface of the world (world courses); `plot_svg.py` draws them.

## Courses (`LEVY_TRAVEL_COURSE`)
- `walk` (default): carved 960-tile surface course. Hills with slopes, a 3-tile ledge, a 14-wide 12-deep dip with a stair out, a forest, a pond, a house with two doors, a mountain with a tunnel, a 62-wide 7-deep lake, a 20-wide chasm with a platform bridge, rough hills. Camps (flat) at offsets 20, 170, 320 ... 920 so a 150-tile leg starts and ends on flat ground.
- `zoo`: one obstacle per 150-tile leg, mostly impassable on foot on purpose: 10-high cliff, 6-wide crack, 14-wide and 20-wide chasms, 12-deep lake, 20-high cliff. Shows what each gear can do.
- `corrupt`: the corruption route, flat camps every 150 from 20 and one obstacle per leg: C0 three sheer holes 4 wide (3, 5, 8 deep), C1 a cave mouth under a rock lid, C2 an 8-wide 60-deep chasm with ledges every 10 rows, C3 a 4-deep hole, C4 a 6-wide 14-deep pit with a sheer near wall and 3-high steps up the far wall.
- `world`: the REAL generated terrain of a world file (`LEVY_TRAVEL_X0` = start column, `FROM`/`TO` are offsets from it, negative goes left). This is the one that reproduced the report; use generated worlds (`gen.sh`), not only carved courses.

## Run
```
export TRAVEL_HOME=~/travel-lab                       # scratch: save/Worlds (copy LevyTest.* and generated M1/M2 here), save/ModSources, tmod/levy.tmod, runs/
./gen.sh 2 11111 M1 7901 ; ./gen.sh 2 22222 M2 7902  # medium worlds, seeds used for the numbers in docs/travel_lab
COURSE=world WORLD=M1 X0=700 ./run.sh tmod/levy.tmod name map 0 800 mixed 15 9000     # tmod name mode from to gear n cap(ticks)   mode: map | follow
LEGACY=1 ./run.sh ...                                 # the same build with the long-trip fixes off (the "before")
./batch.sh $TRAVEL_HOME/tmod/levy.tmod TAG 3          # walk + zoo, long and 150-tile legs, map and follow
./batchW.sh $TRAVEL_HOME/tmod/levy.tmod TAG 3         # two real worlds x five starts: 800 long, follow, three 150 legs
python3 analyze.py TAG [--gear]                       # one table
python3 plot_svg.py out.svg title run_dir [run_dir ...]
```
`LEVY_TRAVEL_CMDFAR` (tiles, default 400) is how far behind the start the commander stands in a map order. A levy spawns in Follow, so with the commander more than 400 tiles from the start it is pulled back to the commander before the order lands (a leg starting past offset 420 of the zoo or corrupt course starts 400 back); the round 2 runs set it to 20.
`round2/` holds the scripts of the second round (`r1.sh` one run, `runall.sh TMOD TAG P SET` the sets, `tab.py TAG...` the before/after table, `sum.py` one line per run); their paths are the lab's own.
Gear (`mixed`, 15 levies): 5 bare, 4 Hermes Boots, 3 Angel Wings, 3 Grappling Hook. `LEVY_TRAVEL_FOES=1` adds day-time monsters ahead of the army and gives them handguns; `LEVY_TRAVEL_PLAN=1` also prints what the route planner finds for each ability class.
The headless server runs the sim far faster than real time (3000 ticks in about 4 s); one run costs the 20 s boot plus a few seconds.

`round3/` is the third round (wall climb gear, off-screen catch-up): `jobs.py TMOD TAG SET` prints one run per line (SET: mixed climb far farclimb near nearclimb zoonear fol), `run_all.sh TMOD TAG sets...` runs them 3 at a time, `r1.sh` keeps only levies.csv and a gzipped events.txt per run, `tabr.py TAG...` and `costs.py TAG PATTERN` read them, `summary.csv` has every levy of the round. Env: LEVY_TRAVEL_HOLD=1 (levies wait where they stand until the order), LEVY_TRAVEL_CMDTRACK=1 (the commander walks after the army), gear climb (Shoe Spikes / Climbing Claws / Tiger Climbing Gear).

`round4/` is the fourth round (hook levies climbing like a player): `jobs.py TMOD TAG SET` (SET: wall zoohook hnear hfar hmed mixednh quick rwq), `hooktab.py TAG [prefix]` one line per run for hook levies, `sumset.py TAG PREFIX` totals, `prog.py` per-run end offsets, `hcost.py` planner and hook cost. New course `LEVY_TRAVEL_COURSE=wall` with `LEVY_TRAVEL_WALLH=H` (a sheer face H tiles tall, plateau on top, start 40, goal 250; negative H = a chasm |H| wide and 60 deep), gear `hooks` (13 levies, one per vanilla hook: Grappling, Amethyst, Sapphire, Emerald, Diamond, Ivy Whip, Web Slinger, Skeletron Hand, Slime Hook, Fish Hook, Dual Hook, Lunar Hook, Static Hook), `LEVY_TRAVEL_NOHOP=1` (no catch-up hop at all: the commander is always on screen). events.txt now carries every hook event (HK lines: plan, cast, latch, release, chain link, hook-jump, landing) and a hookstats line; levies.csv has per-levy hook counters.

`round5/` is the fifth round (lava for every airborne state, grapple more and hold, wing control; results in docs/travel_lab/RESULTS.md round 5): `jobs.py TMOD TAG SET` adds sets `lava5` (cliff over a lava basin with gear `lavamix`, shoves and shaft drop with mixed gear) and `reg` (the quick regression set), `runq.sh TMODNAME TAG SET [ENV=..]` runs a set two at a time with env toggles, `lavasum.py BEFORETAG AFTERTAG` the lost / touched-lava table, `regtab.py` arrivals side by side, `kitsum.py RUN_DIR` one line per boss-fight cell (hook casts per minute, time hooked, wing hold and release, hits, deaths). New env: `LEVY_LAVA_CLIFF=1`, `LEVY_LAVA_GLIDE=1`, `GEAR=lavamix` or `wh`. The boss-fight specs are `lab/specs/travel5_*.yaml`; the switches are `LEVY_AIRLAVA`, `LEVY_WINGCTL`, `LEVY_HOOKMORE`, `LEVY_HM_TRIG`, `LEVY_HM_HANG`, `LEVY_HM_DODGE`, `LEVY_HM_HOLD` (0 turns that part off).

`round6/` is the sixth round (winged levies fly arcs between standing cells instead of dropping into pits; results in docs/travel_lab/RESULTS.md round 6). New courses (`LEVY_TRAVEL_COURSE`): `ravine` (a seeded trek, `LEVY_TRAVEL_SEED`, with valleys with stairs, sheer pits, cracks, hills and mesas whose lip tiles are sloped, half blocks or square; `LEVY_TRAVEL_RAVMODE=walk` keeps only the features a walker can cross) and `lips` (small rises, narrow gaps, shallow dips, mesas and humps whose rim tiles are sloped or half blocks, crossable on foot). Both run from offset 20 to 920 with flat camps every 150, the last 40 tiles flat. Gear `wmix` (Fledgling, Angel, Flame and Steampunk Wings), `wbare` (half bare, half Angel Wings), `w:<ItemID name>` for any one item. The levies.csv has `maxDepth` (tiles below the lower of start and goal), `meanDepth`, `flyShare` (wing thrust ticks), `airShare` (any airborne tick) and `travSec`; events.txt has an `arcs:` line (arcs started, fuel ran out in flight, shouts, flight lines) and the `FEATURES` of the course. `LEVY_TRAVEL_DBG=from,to[,step[,levy]]` prints a DBG line per sampled tick (position, velocity, route state, first waypoint) in events.txt. `LEVY_ARCS=0` turns all the 2.0.53 travel changes off in the same build, `LEVY_ARCSEARCH=0` only the arc search (the old flight grid), `LEVY_NUDGE=0` the shallow-drop nudge, `LEVY_BESTROW=0` the best-effort end penalty, `LEVY_FLIGHTTALK=0` the flight talk. `round6/` has `jobs.py` (sets lipbo, lipb, lipw, ravw, ravb, wrw, wrb), `tab.py` (per-gear table of a tag) and `mkbase.sh` (a "before" build: the old gameplay with the new harness).

`round7/` is the seventh round (the hook levies on the corruption chasm leg; results in docs/travel_lab/RESULTS.md round 7). New env: `LEVY_TRAVEL_MIX=none,boots,wings,hook,...` (the army as a list of gears, levy i gets entry i % count; run.sh variable `MIX`), `LEVY_TRAVEL_NOHOUSE=1` (no house on the walk and corruption courses; on the corruption course it stands across the chasm; run.sh `NOHOUSE=1`), and `LEVY_HOOKDBG=1` now prints the exact position and inputs of every hook plan and a `DOORDBG` line for every door a levy opens (the tML log, `tModLoader-Logs/serverN.log`). `round7/` has `keep.sh` (one chasm-leg run, copies the newest tML log), `matrix.sh` (runs `TAG ENV...` lines 3 at a time through `rgjob2.sh`), `mkrr.py` / `mkrr2.py` (army sizes and noise reruns), `summ7.py BEFORE AFTER` (the round 6 table sets plus the 27-run regression set) and `genall.sh` (regenerates the W3 to W6 worlds).

## Free Rein lab (`freereinlab`)
Measures Free Rein near a base: how far the army strays from its barracks, whether any levy falls into the hole or the elevator shaft or goes underground, what the off-duty pastimes are and what they cost. One yaml, one command:
```
export TRAVELAB_HOME=/home/user/lab/freerein
levy-mod/lab/travel/freereinlab run levy-mod/lab/travel/specs/freerein_base.yaml [--tmod X.tmod] [-j 3] [--keep] [--set runs=4 --set minutes=6]
```
It is the harness scenario `LEVY_HARNESS_SCENARIO=freerein` (`Common/Testing/CombatHarness.FreeRein.cs`). On the generated world it builds, at `runs` base sites 260 tiles apart (the seeds), a pad with a barracks, two campfires, three beds, a keg, chairs and a bench, a 4-wide elevator shaft (a platform every 5 rows) down to a cavern 130 rows under the pad, a sheer 3-wide 30-deep hole and a 9-deep pit with a stair out. Spec keys: `levies` (15, gear 5 bare / 4 boots / 3 wings / 3 hook, or `gear:`), `minutes` per run, `runs`, `shards` (processes per arm), `foes` (day or night monsters around the base and a skeleton in the cavern), `time` (dawn | noon | dusk | midnight), `commander` (base | away | none), `shaft` (platform spacing, 0 bare, -1 none), `life: false` (pastimes off), `arms` (each arm may set `env:` and any key above).
Lab env: `LEVY_FREEREIN_LEGACY=1` is the Free Rein of 2.0.55 in the same build; `LEVY_FREEREIN_ABL` removes parts of the new one (bit flags: 1 old spot pick, 2 no route check, 4 no walk home, 8 old 600-tile circle, 16 no vertical box). The table reports, for the armies of all runs, time inside 150 and 205 tiles, levies ever outside 150, levies that entered the hole / shaft / stair pit, levies 20+ and 40+ rows under the base floor, the pastime mix, and the server cost per tick. Per run, `out/rN/` has `levies.csv`, `events.txt` (the first time each levy left, fell, went deep, with what its brain was doing; `HOLEFALL` blocks), `base.txt` (an ASCII picture of the base) and `trace.txt`.
