# The Cast: every unit, building and enemy the Parliament talks about

Names and numbers for the units, buildings and enemies marked as in the game today were checked against the game's own definitions. Costs are in industrial capacity. Enemy health is the base value, before the fleet's difficulty and time scaling.

A note on names. Three different things are called Leviathan or Tempest. Your Arsenal Ship, your Super Carrier and the enemy Land Leviathan all share a name in part. Tempest is both a rocket artillery truck and a railgun battlecruiser. The papers usually say which one they mean.

## The player's ground units

Infantry is a rifle squad. It costs one hundred, has forty-six health and a range of two hundred sixty. One squad is one army slot, but it carries several bodies, including a dedicated anti-tank and anti-air trooper. In the game today.

Main Battle Tank Aegis is a tank battery with an arcing cannon. It costs one hundred twenty and has one hundred ten health. In the game today.

Heavy Goliath is a breakthrough tank, forged from Heavy Alloy poured by your Factories. It costs two hundred thirty and has two hundred thirty health. It needs the Heavy Alloys research. In the game today.

Mammoth Super-Heavy is a production-line tank that needs a Heavy Foundry and no research. It costs three hundred forty and has three hundred forty health. In the game today.

Anti-Air Hailstorm is a rotary flak gun that sprays tracer fire. It reaches up to the mesosphere and can shoot down falling lances. It costs one hundred and has a range of four hundred forty. In the game today.

Self-Propelled Gun Thunder is slow, long-range artillery. It costs one hundred seventy and reaches seven hundred eighty. In the game today. The docket notes that its shells have no blast radius yet.

Rocket Artillery Tempest fires a tight four-round ripple of fast rockets. It costs one hundred ninety and reaches seven hundred twenty. It needs Rocketry. In the game today.

Hydra Rocket Battery is a saturation rocket truck, built at any Factory once a Missile Works stands. It costs two hundred ten and reaches eight hundred twenty. In the game today.

Surface-to-Air Missile Aegis-AA is a ground missile launcher reaching the mesosphere but not the exosphere. It costs two hundred twenty and reaches six hundred twenty. In the game today.

Railgun Lance is a hypervelocity kinetic gun that reaches any altitude. It needs a Steel deposit and the Kinetic Weapons research. It costs two hundred ninety and reaches one thousand. In the game today.

Veteran Guard is elite armored infantry, built with no research once a Veterans Hall stands. It costs one hundred fifty and has one hundred forty health. In the game today.

Combat Androids are tireless machine infantry that need barely any manpower. They cost one hundred eighty and have one hundred fifty health. They need Robotics. In the game today.

Land Dreadnought is a rolling siege battleship. Its spinal railgun levels ground targets across the map, with flak and a shield for cover. It costs fifteen hundred and has twenty-four hundred health. It is built at the Grand Arsenal. In the game today.

War Colossus is a line fortress. Its plasma slug bursts over a whole cluster, and it carries the largest shield in the game. It costs seventeen hundred fifty and has twenty-eight hundred health. In the game today.

Mobile Command Fortress is a command and area-denial platform. A command bubble boosts nearby fire rate and range. It costs thirteen hundred. In the game today.

## Air

Falcon is the sixth-generation fighter. One crewed jet flies with two wingman drones, and the flight shares one hangar slot. It costs two hundred seventy and reaches seven hundred. It is the most-mentioned unit in the Second Session papers. In the game today.

Strike Jet Warthog is a close-air-support jet with rockets for armor and ships and an autocannon that never runs dry. It costs one hundred seventy and rearms at an airbase. In the game today.

Lancer Heavy Bomber carpet-bombs. It has the longest fuel tank in the fleet. It needs an Aero Works. It costs two hundred eighty. In the game today.

Laser Plane Solaris is a slow, tanky directed-energy gunship with a belly laser. It costs two hundred forty and has no magazine, only fuel. In the game today.

Laser Interceptor Warden is the quickest climber in the fleet, but fragile, with thirty-eight health. It costs one hundred ninety and shoots down nearby missiles. In the game today.

Attack Helicopter Kite hovers over its target with rocket pods. It costs one hundred twenty and has ninety-five health. It cannot reach high altitude. In the game today.

Heavy Gunship Mastodon is a tandem-rotor assault helicopter. It costs three hundred forty, has three hundred eighty health, and uses three rotor slots plus three industrial capacity per second of upkeep. In the game today.

Scout Helicopter Kestrel hunts aircraft with heat-seeking missiles and never strafes the ground. It costs one hundred ten. In the game today.

Command Helicopter Overwatch carries an aura that gives nearby units twenty percent more fire rate and twelve percent more range. It costs one hundred seventy. In the game today.

Transport Helicopter Chinook finds an idle ground unit, carries it to the front and sets it down. It costs one hundred sixty and needs no runway. In the game today.

Combat Drone Squadron is three pilotless drones flying as one unit. It costs two hundred thirty. In the Fusion Age they upgrade to heavy missile drones called Hornets. In the game today.

Kamikaze Drones are cheap suicide swarms that dive into the first enemy they detect. They cost sixty. In the game today.

Nanofortress Airship is a self-repairing flying fortress with a shield and five anti-air turrets. It costs seven hundred and has three thousand health. It is a superweapon. In the game today.

The sky navy is a family of five flying warships, all built at the Sky Dock. They need no hangar slot but have standing upkeep. Sky Patrol Ship costs three hundred and has eight hundred health. Sky Destroyer costs four hundred sixty and has fourteen hundred health. Sky Missile Cruiser costs six hundred forty and reaches into orbit. Sky Railgun Ship costs eight hundred twenty and fires an uninterceptable slug. Nanoforge Carrier is the flagship, at ten hundred fifty, launching eight aircraft and four helicopters. All in the game today.

## Sea

Destroyer Squall is a gun destroyer with laser point defense and a missile turret. It costs two hundred and has one hundred eighty health. In the game today. Note that no enemy currently targets the sea.

Missile Cruiser is a fleet air-defense escort with a long-range missile launcher. It costs two hundred ninety. In the game today.

Carrier Sentinel is a mobile airbase with a free wing of two fighters. It costs three hundred eighty and has five industrial capacity per second upkeep. In the game today.

Arsenal Ship Leviathan is a missile hull with the longest reach in the fleet, at fourteen hundred. It costs four hundred twenty. In the game today.

Railgun Battlecruiser Tempest has a spinal railgun that hits any altitude. It costs four hundred sixty. In the game today.

Grand Battleship is a shore-bombardment dreadnought that out-reaches an orbital rod. It costs fifteen hundred fifty and has twenty-one hundred health. In the game today.

Super Carrier Leviathan-class is a flagship carrier with a free wing of eight fighters. It costs seventeen hundred. In the game today.

## Space

Laser Sat Halo is an orbital laser that dominates high altitude. It costs three hundred twenty and has fifty health. It needs orbital lift and a rocket part. In the game today.

Missile Sat Volley is an orbital missile platform. It costs three hundred and reaches eleven hundred. In the game today.

Tungsten Rods Zeus is an orbital kinetic bombardment platform. It costs three hundred seventy, and each shot does ninety-five damage. In the game today.

Orbital Frigate is an orbital escort whose beam ignores point defence. It costs two hundred. In the game today.

Orbital Gunboat Corsair is a railgun warship above the stratosphere. It costs three hundred forty. In the game today.

Orbital Picket Warden-S is an interceptor satellite that fires one interceptor every two point six seconds. It costs three hundred sixty. In the game today.

Orbital Strike Fighter is a space-superiority interceptor built from a Space Station. It costs two hundred twenty. In the game today.

Antimatter Dreadnought is a slow heavy siege gun in orbit. It costs eight hundred twenty, does three hundred damage a shot, and reaches fourteen hundred. In the game today.

## Exotic units

Shock Troopers are fast, gene-boosted assault infantry. They cost sixty and need Biotechnology. In the game today.

Cybernetic Legion is elite infantry with machine bodies. It costs two hundred and has two hundred thirty health. In the game today.

Clone Battalion is an endless expendable clone squad. It costs twenty-eight and has thirty-four health. In the game today.

Superhuman Cadre is a flying demigod with a reflecting shield and eye beams. It costs seventy and has two hundred twenty health. Each one lowers your stability cap by one and costs two influence per second. In the game today.

Psi-Troopers are mind-warriors hurling psychic plasma at any altitude. They cost one hundred fifty and reach five hundred twenty. In the game today.

Telekinetic Pylon is a mind-force superweapon that works at any altitude. It costs five hundred sixty and reaches eleven hundred fifty. In the game today.

Shapeshifters are a living nanoswarm that becomes anything. They cost three hundred and have two hundred sixty health. The Parliament ruled that their self-repair shuts off in heat. In the game today.

Titan is a living colossus with a plasma siege cannon. It costs seven hundred sixty and has thirteen hundred health. In the game today.

Colossus Walker is a towering battle-walker with a laser cannon reaching the mesosphere. It costs six hundred twenty and has nine hundred health. In the game today.

## Buildings

Radar boosts nearby unit range by twelve percent, and it stacks. It also nudges your stability cap up. It costs one hundred. In the game today.

Data Center costs heavy manpower up front. After that, drones need none, and research runs forty percent faster. In the game today.

Rail Hub is an underground rail node. Idle troops dive in and redeploy at rail speed. It costs two hundred twenty. In the game today.

Space Center produces orbital units and gives five orbital lift. It also builds rocket parts. It costs two hundred eighty. In the game today.

Space Station gives eight orbital lift and lets space units skip the launch at thirty percent off. It costs four hundred forty. In the game today.

Power Plant gives seven industrial capacity per second. It costs one hundred twenty. In the game today.

Extractor goes on a resource deposit and claims a special resource plus industrial capacity. It costs ninety. In the game today.

Missile Silo is the only strategic launcher today. It enables the strike button. It costs four hundred twenty. In the game today.

Psionic Relay projects fire rate and range like a radar, but its mind-static lowers your stability cap and drains influence. It costs one hundred eighty. In the game today.

Mass-Driver Railway is a railgun emplacement. Place several in a row and each adjacent segment hits harder. It costs three hundred sixty. In the game today.

Orbital Gun is a giant surface cannon that hurls rounds into the upper atmosphere. It costs four hundred sixty. In the game today.

Aero Works is an aircraft production line. It gives four air command and lets any Airbase build the Lancer and Laser Plane. It costs two hundred forty. In the game today.

Sky Dock is the only place sky warships are built. It costs four hundred forty. In the game today.

Research Lab makes research faster and cheaper in influence, and it stacks. It costs one hundred seventy. In the game today.

Anti-Missile Interceptor is a battery with a magazine of four, reloading one every ten seconds. It costs five hundred. In the game today.

Shield Generator projects a dome that rods, missiles and bombs splash against. It costs three hundred twenty. In the game today.

Particle Cannon fires a single near-instant bolt at any altitude. It costs four hundred forty. In the game today.

## Enemy units

Drone is the basic hostile swarm unit. It has sixteen health and dives at targets from the troposphere. In the game today.

Bombpod is a falling re-entry pod that dives into the ground. It has about ten health. In the game today.

Interceptor is a fast enemy fighter with air-to-air rockets. It has twenty-two health. In the game today.

Bomber is a high enemy bomber that sits in the stratosphere. It has fifty-two health and armor five. Only aircraft and railguns can reach it. In the game today.

Gunship is an enemy low-altitude strafer with thirty health. In the game today.

Strikewing is a shielded rail fighter. The shield soaks guns and lasers, and only missiles overload it. In the game today.

Raider is an agile twin-railgun dogfighter. In the game today.

Escort Fighter is a rail fighter that flies with bosses. In the game today.

Frigate is a slow missile platform in the upper atmosphere. It has seventy health, out of reach of most surface-to-air missiles. In the game today.

Whistler is an antimatter drone with six rounds, then it dives and detonates. In the game today.

Ground Tank and Ground Mech are the hostile landing forces. They have seventy and one hundred health. In the game today.

Pelter is ground plasma artillery with fifty-five health and a shot every seven seconds. In the game today.

Earthbreaker is a heavy seismic press with six hundred twenty health and armor twenty. Its earthquakes hurt morale and nearby buildings. In the game today.

Shield Generator Pod is an enemy that drops with the wave and becomes a landed structure projecting a dome. Kill it to drop the shield. In the game today.

Shield Carrier is a flying shield ship that follows your densest anti-air cluster and projects a moving dome. In the game today.

Jammer is a drone with no guns whose field blinds your homing missiles. Kill it with guns, lasers or kinetics. In the game today.

Hijacker is a fast, shielded stand-off drone that works against your swarms. In the game today.

Breacher is a landed anchor with a deflector field that scrubs your projectiles and fabricates reinforcements. It has three hundred health. In the game today.

Bulwark is an exosphere heavy platform with armor twenty. It lobs plasma at the ground. Only space weapons and orbital guns reach it. In the game today.

Railsat, Lasersat and Mender are the grid of orbital satellites. Railsats hit hard, lasersats are constant pickets, and menders heal every hostile in reach. In the game today.

## Enemy superweapons

Skyfortress Hive is an airborne drone carrier with eleven hundred health and armor fourteen. It sits in the stratosphere and produces more enemies. In the game today.

Land Leviathan is a ground siege walker with seventeen hundred health and armor twenty-two. In the game today.

Orbital Annihilator has nine hundred fifty health and sits in space. It charges a beam for six seconds. A hit during the charge interrupts it. In the game today.

Autodrone Bombarder has fifteen hundred health and armor sixteen. It is anchored in space, produces more enemies and bombards. In the game today.

Fleet Flagship Sovereign is the carrier at the heart of the fleet. It has nine thousand health, armor thirty and a shield of up to three thousand. It arrives with bombarders, pickets and a satellite screen. In the game today.

## Proposed new units and buildings from the parliament papers

Reaver is an enemy air-superiority hunter that goes after your fighters. The air council proposed it. It would spawn once you field twenty fighters.

Weasel is an enemy anti-radiation flight that homes on radars. The air council proposed it.

Durandal is an enemy bomber that cracks runways so aircraft cannot launch or land. The air council and the autofleet council proposed it.

Hardened Shelter is a building that protects parked aircraft from runway attacks. The air council proposed it.

Argus is an unarmed airborne radar plane with a datalink aura reaching eleven hundred. The air council proposed it, and the space-fleet council backed it.

Hush-Hound is a light, fast enemy with sixty health. It carries a field that halves psi fire rate and range. The psi council proposed it.

Starfish is a high-altitude nuclear burst that blinds the fleet's electronics and your own unhardened ones. The physics council proposed it, with the missiles council.

Retrograde Shotgun is a satellite that fires an uninterceptable lane of pellets through the thermosphere. The space-fleet council proposed it. It would cost three hundred eighty.

Kessler Doctrine is an opt-in choice that burns the thermosphere for a hundred eighty seconds. The physics, space-fleet and market councils proposed it.

Helios Array is a solar satellite that gives income and powers nearby orbital weapons. The space-fleet council proposed it. It costs four hundred twenty.

Orion Battleship Pusher is a nuclear-pulse space capital ship with four thousand health. It carries ten Orbital Strike Fighters. The space-fleet council and the megaprojects council proposed it.

Molodets is a strategic missile launcher that rides the rail network. The command council proposed it.

Boomer is a ballistic missile submarine in a new depth band. The missiles council proposed it.

Dipping-sonar Kite is a helicopter that hunts submarines. The air council proposed it.

Trench Lander is an enemy that anchors at sea level and builds hunters. It has nine hundred health. The naval council proposed it.

Sapper is a combat engineer who lays mines and fortifies. It costs ninety. The ground council proposed it.

Sanitiser is an enemy drone that detonates wrecks. The industry council proposed it.

Mirror echoes are enemy copies of your top-killing unit. The autofleet council proposed them.

Planning Bureau is a building that runs your ledger of standing orders. The command council proposed it.

Tracking Array is a building that shows the invasion's arrival within five seconds. The pacing council proposed it.

Listening Chamber is a building that gives a ninety-second look at the fleet's plan at a stability cost. The psi council proposed it.

Staff Bunker is a deep command post that keeps government running. The command council proposed it.

Reconstruction Corps is a rail-linked crew that rebuilds destroyed buildings. The industry council proposed it.

Radiotroph Garden turns fallout into industrial capacity, influence and healing. It costs one hundred sixty. The bio council proposed it.

Launch Rail is a long barrel that shoots hardened satellites into orbit with no rocket part. It costs five hundred. The railgun council proposed it.

Long Gun is a superheavy railgun built section by section, with each section costing three hundred eighty. The megaprojects council proposed it, with the railgun council.

Meridian Accelerator is a doctrine that turns a chain of rail hubs and mass drivers into one giant gun. The railgun council proposed it.

Ammunition Train is a supply convoy that makes guns fire faster while burning industrial capacity. The industry council proposed it.

Warhead Stockpile is a magazine of strategic warheads that replaces the strike cooldown. The missiles council proposed it.

Delta Works are seawalls and polders for holding land below sea level. The planet council proposed them.
