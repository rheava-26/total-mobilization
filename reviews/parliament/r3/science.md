# SCIENCE COUNCIL — Round 3 Paper (`science`)

## 1. Answers

**HOT GROUND (bio; to industry, science) — AMEND.** Co-sign with one amendment. The fallout-husbandry doctrine registers in the shared `DOCTRINES` table (MEGA-1 container) and runs on the once-per-second doctrine tick, not per chunk. Chunk yield is summed per garden in that tick, to protect performance. Everything else as written, including taboo strikes at full stability cost.

**PHASED ARRAYS, METERED (energy; to science) — ACCEPT.** The 0.4 IC/s flat upkeep is dropped in favour of the ENERGY-2 power bill, which is the better rule. +3% per emitter stays uncapped. The +15% heat at 10 or more emitters is welcome. Node: `phasedarrays`, pre `directedenergy` + `automation`, tier 2, cost 220, not dang.

**THE WRECK DOCTRINE (industry; to science, lore) — AMEND.** Co-sign into a single coalition bill with THE DOSSIER LINE (lore). One record per kind (`G.codex[kind]`), two recovery routes (Salvage Crawler/Yard, or a wreck within 500 px of a Research Lab), and both routes pay both prizes: alloy plus a Sample plus the dossier. Without that, the player has to pick between salvage and science, which is a denial.

**THE DOSSIER LINE (lore; to science, industry) — ACCEPT.** This is the most complete of the three wreck motions and is the version we back. Only the `warp` sample is a hard gate (the Return Address). Sample discount is −12% per kind on `studies` nodes, floor 40% of base.

**THIN AIR (physics; to science) — AMEND.** Co-sign with two amendments. First, `ionoheat` prerequisites are `spaceage` + `directedenergy` only. `geosurvey` is withdrawn (see below), so it cannot be a prerequisite. Second, satellite decay stays HP-only with the cheap Reboost pulse, as filed, which keeps Article IV.2 intact. Numbers: 6 px/s descent per section, uncapped; radar −25%; 2 IC/s per section; cost ~430, dang, sc 8.

**DEPOSIT GRADE, DEPTH AND CASCADE (planet; to science, industry) — AMEND.** The grade mechanic is Planet's and is credited to PLANET-3; Science has withdrawn `geosurvey`. Amendment: our `egs` (Enhanced Geothermal) is merged into Planet's `deepcore` node (pre `fusion` + `deeprail`) as its first rung, so there is one geothermal node, not two. Buried plants doubling output and immune to carpet bombardment stays. The Seismic Survey Station reveals grades during prep.

**THE LISTENING (psi; to market, science) — ACCEPT.** The costly active peek at 18 stability cap for 90 s is a clean price. Our survey research supplies the ground truth `n_obs` that makes the Chamber's reveal accurate. The `listenDebt` ratchet reading back to the fleet is exactly the escalation the tenet wants.

## 3. Competing motions

- **Forecast (three versions: ONE DIAL, FIVE FACES from new-player; THE ONE FORECAST from pacing; our UNIFIED FORECAST).** We back **ONE DIAL, FIVE FACES**. It carries one `G.forecast` object, the `1/sqrt(n)` statistic, a new `signals` node, and five renderers, so Market's panel, Lore's Wire and Pacing's beats are all faces of one object. Its combo `signals` + `automation` is our doctrine; we ask that it be renamed **Synoptic Survey** so that "Deep Survey" belongs to Planet alone. We withdraw our own Unified Forecast in its favour.
- **Wreck economy (three versions: Wreck Doctrine, Dossier Line, Wreck Codex).** We back **THE DOSSIER LINE** with the amendment above. It already merges Lore's dossier, Industry's salvage and our samples, and names the Sanitiser escalation.
- **Ionospheric heating (physics THIN AIR, our BREATHING SKY).** We back **THIN AIR**, with the prerequisites amended as above. Physics owns the megaproject and the aurora HUD.

## 4. Closing statement

Members, the Academy came to this Parliament with five bills and a catapult, and we leave with a single dossier line, a single forecast, a single heated sky and a single geothermal node. We withdrew three nodes and lost nothing. That is how science actually works: you publish, you are scooped, you co-author. The tree will now do what the designer asked of it from the first day, which is to change the world and not just the unit list. We ask only that nobody shorten the prep clock, and that somebody, someday, build the Return Address.
